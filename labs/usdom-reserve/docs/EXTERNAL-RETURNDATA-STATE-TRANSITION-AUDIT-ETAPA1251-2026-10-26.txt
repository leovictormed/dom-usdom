ETAPA 1251 — external returndata and state-transition audit
Date: 2026-10-26

Scope
-----

Adversarial review of every remaining Solidity call boundary and the state
transitions surrounding it: Safe identity/policy getters, finalized-burn and
checkpoint proof adapters, registry reads, USDT policy/balance/transfer calls,
queue mutation, settlement, pause/resume and terminal shutdown. No Rust,
production script, network, signing, deployment, funds, commit or push operation
was performed.

Result
------

No new material authorization, accounting, reentrancy or returndata-copy defect
was found.

- Every Safe getter has a fixed gas cap, copies at most its expected fixed
  response and checks exact returndata length plus canonical offset/count/value.
- Verifier/config/capability and token/registry scalar calls copy one word and
  reject every other length. Proof verifier calls that must return nothing copy
  zero bytes and require zero returndata.
- Token mutations accept only the historical USDT empty return or an exact
  canonical `true`; failure, false, short, trailing and return-bomb responses
  revert the complete transaction.
- External proof and identity interactions are STATICCALLs. They cannot mutate
  or reenter state transitions. Deposit, queue and payout token callbacks are
  protected by `nonReentrant`, and every failed postcondition rolls back replay,
  debt, queue, bucket and accounting writes.
- Queue removal validates accounting, generation, head/tail and reciprocal
  links before writes. Proved debt is reduced only after an exact permanent
  burn/request/debt/finality match and remains unchanged by pause, expiry or
  pruning.
- The stage-1244 deployment-chain correction remains effective; current-chain
  drift cannot select a weaker Safe policy.

Regression expansion
--------------------

The test Safe now supplies adversarial auxiliary responses independently for:

- `getThreshold()`: 31-byte short, 33-byte trailing and one-megabyte bomb;
- `getModulesPaginated()`: 95-byte short, 97-byte trailing, one-megabyte bomb
  and wrong dynamic offset with an otherwise exact response length; and
- `getStorageAt()`: 95-byte short, 97-byte trailing, one-megabyte bomb and wrong
  dynamic byte length with an otherwise exact response length.

New Reserve and Registry regressions exercise all eleven malformed modes through
their public health predicates and prove exact restoration. The stage-1244
owner tests continue to cover revert, short/trailing/bomb, wrong offset/count
and dirty address padding. The new code is confined to test mocks and tests;
production runtime semantics and ABI did not change in this stage.
