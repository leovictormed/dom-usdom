Stage 491 — Deployment handoff observation-SHA package binding
Date: 2026-10-23

The deployment transaction handoff accepted the identity observation SHA-256
through the environment even though the reviewed deployment package already
commits that exact value. This duplicated semantic identity at the Ledger
boundary and allowed callers to provide a second, independently mutable claim.

The handoff now authenticates and parses the reviewed package first, derives
the required observation SHA-256 from its observationDocumentSha256 field, and
then authenticates the direct observation bytes. Source package rederivation
receives only the package-derived value. The executor no longer requires the
redundant environment variable. Substitution or omission fails closed against
the reviewed package hash and its embedded observation identity.

`production_deployment_handoff_observation_sha_derived_from_package` is a
structural fact. No network access, hardware signature, transaction, funds,
mining, commit or push occurred while producing this evidence.

Validation: deployment identity and complete runbook suites 78/78; readiness
60/60.
