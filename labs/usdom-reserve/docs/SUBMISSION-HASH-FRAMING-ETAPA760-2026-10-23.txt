Finding and correction
======================

Recovery previously obtained the persisted submission hash through a generic
line reader. Although that reader had recently become stricter, the hash
artifact did not have a shared format-specific writer/reader validator.

A bounded private-file helper now accepts exactly 67 bytes: lowercase `0x`, 64
lowercase hexadecimal digits and one LF. It rejects CRLF, missing/final extra
newline, additional lines, whitespace, uppercase, wrong length and nonhex
bytes. The writer validates the temporary artifact before create-once install;
current rereads and partial recovery use the same helper.

The extracted hash remains linked to raw-validation transaction hash, queried
transaction/receipt, normalized receipt projection and completion manifest.
All evidence files remain snapshotted and byte-rechecked around RPC activity.
