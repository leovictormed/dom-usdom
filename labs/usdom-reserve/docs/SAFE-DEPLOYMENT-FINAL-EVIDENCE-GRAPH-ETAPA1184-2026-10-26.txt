SAFE DEPLOYMENT FINAL EVIDENCE GRAPH — STAGE 1184
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The final Safe deployment barrier reread receipt and validation artifacts but
did not reread the signed raw transaction or its create-once journal. Those
files had been validated earlier per role and could change while later roles
were processed. Success could therefore coexist with a corrupted durable
resume graph even though each on-chain Safe and receipt remained correct.

Correction
==========

For every role with any local deployment evidence, the final barrier now
requires journal, raw transaction, validation and receipt as one complete set.
It privately rereads all four, proves the journal embeds the exact raw and
validation content, re-runs the strict raw-transaction validator for that role
and nonce, then reprojects the receipt. Each canonical output must equal its
installed bytes. A permissionlessly deployed exact Safe remains supported only
when all four local evidence files are absent; any partial graph fails closed.
