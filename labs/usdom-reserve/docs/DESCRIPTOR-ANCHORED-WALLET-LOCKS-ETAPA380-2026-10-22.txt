USDOM stage 380 — descriptor-anchored wallet locks
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

Wallet snapshot and TPM authority locks validated an owner-only parent and
then opened the lock by its pathname. A same-user concurrent process could
replace a pathname component inside that interval. Post-open inode checks
detected most substitutions, but the authority was not continuously anchored
to the directory descriptor used for lookup.

Correction
==========

dom-wallet-crypto now exposes open_private_lock_file. On Unix it retains and
authenticates the exact mode-0700 current-user parent, resolves the child with
openat plus O_NOFOLLOW and O_CLOEXEC, creates it exclusively at mode 0600 or
opens the existing direct file, synchronizes creation, and authenticates the
descriptor-relative name, original path, owner, mode, link count, device and
inode before returning the handle.

SnapshotLock and the shared TPM authority/keystore lock path now use this one
implementation. The verification-only checkpoint planner also drops verified
receipt objects immediately; it retains them only when the prove feature needs
them as recursive assumptions.
