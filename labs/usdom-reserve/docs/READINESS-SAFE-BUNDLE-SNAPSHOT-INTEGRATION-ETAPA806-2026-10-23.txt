ETAPA 806 — aggregate readiness tests and Safe bundle snapshot integration
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The aggregate configured/unconfigured readiness and preflight suites passed, but
an inventory of the real readiness checker found a consumer integration gap.
`safeRecoveryFinalEvidenceBundle` was mandatory in the release schema and pure
assessment, yet `check-mainnet-readiness.mjs` omitted it from the document list
snapshotted into `evidenceFileBytes`. Its later strict parser therefore always
received no bytes. A genuinely configured run would fail as if the bundle were
missing even when its path and SHA-256 were correct.

Correction

- Added the final Safe recovery bundle to the checker's hash-addressed document
  snapshot list before any semantic parsing.
- The existing strict parser now consumes the exact bytes whose SHA-256 was
  recorded, preserving the direct-file and single-snapshot boundary.
- Added a structural regression requiring both snapshot admission and semantic
  parsing of that evidence slot.

Blocker inventory

- The unconfigured checked-in candidate remains deliberately blocked on real
  operator decisions, signatures, deployment evidence and funding authority.
- No additional schema/evidence defect was observed in the aggregate suites.
- The fixed gap was an implementation blocker for configured readiness, not a
  decision or funds blocker.

Validation

- Initial aggregate readiness/preflight/release-schema suite: 70/70 pass.
- Post-fix focused plus aggregate suite: 76/76 pass.
- No RPC, build, deployment, signature, commit, push or funds operation ran.
