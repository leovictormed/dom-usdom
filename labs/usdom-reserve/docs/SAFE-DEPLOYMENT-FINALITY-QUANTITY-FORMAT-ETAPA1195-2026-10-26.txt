SAFE DEPLOYMENT FINALITY QUANTITY FORMAT — STAGE 1195
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

Stage 1177 correctly removed RPC-derived values from Bash arithmetic, but its
inline validator required both heights to be decimal. The strict receipt
projector intentionally preserves Ethereum's canonical hexadecimal quantity
for `blockNumber` (`0x10`), while Cast's finalized block field is decimal. Every
real receipt would therefore fail the finality barrier despite valid evidence.

Correction
==========

A dedicated checker now parses the finalized height as bounded canonical
decimal and the receipt block as bounded canonical Ethereum quantity. Both are
limited to uint64 before `BigInt` comparison. The checker rejects insufficient
finality, mixed formats, leading zeros, overflow and a 100,000-digit input. It
is included in the critical publication inventory.
