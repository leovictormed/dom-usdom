USDOM canonical V2 ingress and restart recovery — stage 151
Date: 2026-10-18

Purpose
=======

Stage 150 persisted the V1-to-V2 activation boundary but the live ChainState
could not atomically accept a candidate-v2 package. This stage implements the
first canonical direct-extension path. It does not activate a public network.

Canonical V2 persistence
========================

StateCommitmentV2 now has a fixed-order, versioned UDSTV002 encoding. Decode
validates the complete bridge identity, monetary and authenticated-set
invariants, rejects truncation and trailing bytes, and re-encodes exactly.

UDV2W001 carries the ordered bounded transition witnesses used by one block.
Its four-MiB limit leaves room for the separately bounded proof envelope in the
store's 16-MiB atomic extension-state transaction. The witness commitment is
included in the opaque verified candidate produced after stateless execution.

Every accepted V2 block persists, atomically with the DOM block and ordinary
UTXO/kernel changes:

- the exact proof envelope and package commitment;
- the canonical witness bundle and commitment;
- the previous and next complete V2 commitments;
- the version-3 transition receipt and header commitment;
- the ratified UDACTV02 schedule commitment; and
- a canonical runtime-tip marker.

Restart restoration reads the canonical block/tip relation, reconstructs the
package and witnesses, verifies every persisted commitment, reruns the proof
verifiers and stateless transition, and requires the same receipt and next
state before returning a trusted V2 tip.

ChainState direct ingress
=========================

connect_usdom_v2_package_for_schedule accepts only the next canonical block
under the exact network, chain and migration schedule. It validates parent
linkage, timestamps, PoW, target, accumulated work, fork choice, ordinary DOM
inputs, output uniqueness, kernel replay, proof envelope, V2 witnesses and the
header-bound transition receipt. All state is committed in one LMDB
transaction. Caller state advances only after that commit succeeds.

The integration vector installs V1 and V2 activation records at genesis,
rejects a substituted schedule, connects the first V2 block, detects an exact
duplicate, closes and reopens LMDB, replays recovery verification, opens
ChainState again and connects the second V2 block.

Security boundary and remaining work
====================================

This stage makes candidate-v2 canonical direct extensions and restart recovery
real ChainState operations. The node runtime still selects the V1 full-state
package path. Production still requires:

1. A V2 sidecar carrying both proof envelope and witness bundle, retained side
   branches, full V2 fork-choice promotion and rollback in one transaction.
2. V2 package/body IBD, peer relay, orphan handling, mining and node startup
   selection wired to the persisted UDACTV02 schedule.
3. A real finalized Ethereum reserve-deposit witness through issuance, private
   transfer, burn, finalized-burn proof and Ethereum payout.
4. Production proving artifacts, ceremonies/verifying keys, gas benchmarks,
   ratified operations and independent audits.

Public-network pins remain unset and readiness gates remain fail closed. No
push, commit, deployment, transaction or use of funds was performed.
