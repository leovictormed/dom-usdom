USD-native / USDOM — Stage 426: proof manifest canonical size
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

After the production adapters were restricted to the canonical 260-byte RISC
Zero Groth16 EVM seal, the finalized-burn and checkpoint verifier manifests
still declared maxProofBytes=65536. The contracts rejected the excess bytes,
but a release reviewer could sign a proof-system policy that overstated the
accepted encoding and disagreed with the executable boundary.

Correction
==========

Both verifier-configuration validators now require maxProofBytes=260, derived
from the same four-byte selector plus eight 32-byte BN254 words used by their
submission validators. The checked-in unconfigured manifests, capacity
collectors and all release/test fixtures use that exact value. Values of 259
and 261 are independently rejected. Artifact collectors also reject a verifier
configuration whose declared maximum differs from the canonical seal size.
