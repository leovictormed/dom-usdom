Stage 212 — historical irreversible burn under fresh Ethereum finality
Date: 2026-10-20

Purpose

Stage 210 made an expired Ethereum queue lease renewable. This must be supported
by the production proof relation, not only by the Solidity queue. A stale native
validUntil must remain authenticated as part of the original burn intent while a
new proof binds the same burn-set inclusion to a recent finalized Beacon slot.

Verified relation

The finalized-burn program already enforces the required separation:

- BurnStateWitness authenticates the permanent burn record and its original
  validUntil under the native USDOM state root;
- the verified checkpoint authenticates that same state root through the
  Ethereum-finalized checkpoint-registry event;
- finalityBeaconSlot is taken from the current verified finality head;
- no comparison treats the historical validUntil as a forfeiture condition;
- the resulting Solidity-compatible statement binds both the unchanged native
  intent deadline and the fresh finality slot.

Regression vector

The new test creates a valid irreversible burn, verifies its original checkpoint,
then moves Ethereum finality 30 days past the burn's validUntil. It advances the
finalized Beacon slot and execution identity while preserving the exact DOM
checkpoint record and native burn witness. Verification must succeed, produce a
different statement from the original finality head and equal the independently
reconstructed statement containing:

- the original burnId, requestId, recipient, amount and validUntil;
- the original authenticated DOM block/state identity;
- the new finalized Beacon slot;
- the same chain, reserve and activation-schedule bindings.
