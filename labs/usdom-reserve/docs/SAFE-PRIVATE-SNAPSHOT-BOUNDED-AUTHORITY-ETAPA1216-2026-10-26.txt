SAFE PRIVATE SNAPSHOT BOUNDED AUTHORITY — STAGE 1216
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

Every resumable and final Safe evidence read passes through `snapshot`, but the
function directly streamed the generic private reader's 16 MiB allowance into
its destination without an outer timeout or private stderr. The artifacts in
this ceremony have substantially smaller semantic bounds.

Correction
==========

All snapshots now reattest Node and timeout, run under a 30-second TERM/KILL
deadline and capture stderr privately. Fatal UTF-8 decoding is required;
artifact output is capped at 2 MiB and diagnostics at 64 KiB. Success requires
nonempty output, empty stderr and mode 0600 before bytes reach comparison,
parsing, resume reconstruction or the final evidence barrier.
