ETAPA 839 — readiness child process-group lifecycle
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

Readiness children had output bounds and executable rechecks but no explicit
timeout in the local executor. Killing a Forge parent alone is also insufficient:
compiler grandchildren can survive if the invoker dies or a limit is exceeded.
This matched the observed orphaned Forge/I/O condition.

Correction

- Replaced the readiness-local exec wrapper with the shared pinned child runner.
- Every child starts in a dedicated process group with stdin closed and separate
  bounded stdout/stderr.
- Timeout or output overflow sends TERM to the entire group, schedules KILL after
  two seconds, and waits for child/pipe close before returning. `finally` kills
  any remaining group and rechecks executable identity.
- Readiness now declares explicit deadlines: Forge/Cargo ten minutes; every
  Git/Python query thirty seconds.
- The four reproducible-build preparers also declare their ten-minute Forge
  deadline explicitly; no caller may omit timeout or output bounds.

Validation

- An adversarial test starts a shell grandchild, forces timeout and verifies the
  descendant PID no longer exists; output-flood termination is also covered.
- Syntax and focused lifecycle/readiness/reproducible-build tests: 16/16 pass.
- Diff check passes.
- No Forge/Cargo build, RPC, deploy, signature, commit, push or funds action ran.
