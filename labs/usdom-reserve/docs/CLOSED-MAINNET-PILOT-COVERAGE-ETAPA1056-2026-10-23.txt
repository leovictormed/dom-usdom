  record and allowance reset;

  burn/request replay flags;

  idempotent;

Remaining evidence, not missing implementation
----------------------------------------------
1. Real RPC agreement, finalized receipts and canonical block hashes can exist only
   during the authorized mainnet ceremony.
2. Ledger identity/signature behavior and physical separation require the operator's
   hardware; no secret was generated or requested here.
3. Exact live USDT balances, allowance behavior, blacklist/pause policy and reserve
   state require read-only/live observations followed by explicitly authorized funds.
4. Proof production and verifier acceptance require the separately running RISC Zero
   release evidence. This inventory does not substitute for it.
5. Two public RPC URLs reduce a single-endpoint fault but cannot prove organizational
   independence or eliminate common upstream correlation. Any disagreement fails
   closed; operator source diversification remains an operational prerequisite.
6. Unrelated balance transfers during baseline windows deliberately stop exact-delta
   reconciliation and require investigation/restart; they are not auto-accepted.
