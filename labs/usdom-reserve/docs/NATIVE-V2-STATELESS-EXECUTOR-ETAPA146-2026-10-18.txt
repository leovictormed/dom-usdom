USDOM candidate-v2 stateless native executor — stage 146
Date: 2026-10-18

Purpose
=======

Stage 145 authenticated every native replay-protection and Ethereum-identity
set, but its sparse and accumulator witnesses still advanced separate parts of
the candidate state. This stage composes those openings with the complete
state-dependent transition relation and binds the supplied decomposition to an
independently authenticated previous root.

Complete transition witness
===========================

NativeTransitionWitness combines UDAFRT01 and UDNSET01. UDNVW001 is its unique
bounded transport: an eight-byte magic, exact u32 lengths, and the two canonical
component encodings. Decoding rejects oversized input, arithmetic overflow,
truncation, trailing data, altered lengths, invalid component encodings and any
representation that does not re-encode byte for byte.

Keccak-256 of the frozen initial-issuance UDNVW001 vector:
  af43c6bc5675031cdd5a8844ec89cebe07dd8e444d3f45876ea4f58b4f310487

Authenticated execution
=======================

apply_v2 requires the expected previous root separately and rejects zero or a
decomposition whose DOM:usdom-state:v2 hash differs. It checks structural v2
invariants before and after execution, including note/set counts, burn/request
counts, supply bounds, anchor-history bounds, issuance identity counts, all
nonzero component roots, immutable bridge policy and Ethereum chain identity.

After the transition has been converted into an opaque VerifiedTransition by
the deposit, Ethereum-finality or private-spend verifier, the stateless executor
reproduces the full state-dependent rules:

* issuance uniqueness, note insertion, amount accounting, Ethereum genesis,
  execution/Beacon identity consistency and high-water advancement;
* transfer nullifier/note uniqueness, canonical shape and retained input anchor;
* burn uniqueness, retained anchor, canonical shape, bridge target, private
  burn-id derivation, supply bound, Ethereum freshness, clock margins, deadline
  bounds and exact Solidity obligation commitment; and
* checkpoint genesis identity, monotonicity and strict advancement.

It advances PMMR, Poseidon and retained-anchor frontiers and all seven sparse
roots without historical notes or complete sets. Tests compare the entire
resulting StateCommitmentV2 byte-for-byte with the full-state executor for all
four transition kinds.

Ordered block batches
=====================

apply_v2_transition_batch executes up to the consensus maximum of 1,024
transitions, chaining every returned root into the next witness. The generator
constructs witnesses against a cloned full state and applies the same ordered
batch while producing them. Tests prove equality with atomic full-state batch
execution and reject missing or reordered witnesses. Empty batches still
authenticate the supplied previous root and validate the v2 decomposition.

Security boundary and remaining work
====================================

The executor accepts only opaque VerifiedTransition capabilities; raw deposit,
checkpoint or private proof bytes cannot construct them. This stage does not
activate v2 or change the current v1 consensus root. Production still requires:

1. Expose an equally bounded proof-verified block-envelope capability so the
   checkpoint program can use UDNVW001 without opening NativeUsdomState.
2. Freeze and test the v1-to-v2 activation snapshot, header commitment version,
   persistence/rebuild procedure and reorg behavior.
3. Select and benchmark the production proving backend, complete the 360+30
   positive checkpoint vector and publish immutable verifier/key artifacts.
4. Ratify production identities, limits and incident procedures, then obtain
   independent audits and complete a capped public rehearsal.

No push, deployment, transaction or use of funds was performed.
