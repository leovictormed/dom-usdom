Stage 181 - RISC Zero RandomX receipt assumption boundary
Date: 2026-10-19

Purpose

UDCSEG02 stores a 32-byte RISC Zero ImageID and the RandomX receipt journal is
the 32-byte UDCRXST1 commitment. RISC Zero internally represents Digest as
eight u32 words. Converting through host integer notation or reversing each word
would request a receipt for another ImageID even though the displayed hex looked
related. The exact conversion and guest call therefore need one owned boundary.

Implemented boundary

1. The optional risc0-recursion feature pins risc0-zkvm exactly to 3.0.6 and
   does not enter the default portable dependency graph.
2. checkpoint_randomx_receipt_image_id_digest uses RISC Zero's direct
   [u8; 32] conversion. No integer parser, host endian conversion or word
   reversal occurs.
3. On target_os=zkvm only,
   verify_checkpoint_randomx_receipt_assumption_in_guest calls env::verify with
   the state-pinned Digest and the exact raw 32-byte UDCRXST1 commitment.
4. The guest-only cfg prevents a native host call from being represented as
   successful recursive receipt verification.
5. The byte-order vector 00 01 ... 1f round-trips through Digest::as_bytes and
   displays as the same canonical 64 hexadecimal digits. Its first word is also
   checked against native in-memory byte order.

Verification

- 20 risc0-recursion portable tests pass;
- default portable, host-reference and all-feature profiles remain covered;
- strict all-target/all-feature Clippy, rustfmt and dependency graph checks are
  recorded in results-stage181-2026-10-19.

Security status

This implements the exact recursive assumption call site and removes byte-order
ambiguity. It does not provide the receipt being assumed: the RandomX guest,
its independently audited ImageID, proving capacity and segment guest are still
absent. A conditional receipt with an unresolved assumption must never be sent
to Ethereum as a complete proof. Mainnet and real funds remain fail closed. No
deploy, public transaction, funds, commit or push occurred. The node and miner
remained stopped.
