Classification

- Private plaintext: 10 source-read buffers — 2 checkpoint private inputs, 4

  tempfile or mmap is used in these reads; public receipts remain durable.

Correction and limits

  depend on workspace zeroize. Public artifacts are not unnecessarily erased.
- Zeroization limits heap lifetime but cannot guarantee exclusion from swap or
  a privileged memory/core capture; launcher core/dump controls remain the
  corresponding process boundary.
