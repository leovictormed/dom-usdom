ETAPA 739 — signing ceremony SHA-256 executable pinning
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
The three signing ceremonies used PATH-resolved `sha256sum` and `awk` to establish and recheck
the hashes of Cast, Node and JQ. Replacement after consent could make all executable hash checks
accept a substituted tool.

Correction
==========
Each ceremony now resolves `sha256sum` once, validates its path, file/link/owner/mode/directory
properties, snapshots metadata and its self-hash, and rechecks these before hashing Cast, Node or
JQ. A shell reader validates the exact lowercase 64-hex digest, removing `awk` from the trust path.
Checkpoint request/signature hashes also use the pinned helper.

Validation
==========
Combined structural tests pass 3/3. All three scripts pass `bash -n`; Node syntax and diff checks
pass. No ceremony, Ledger, RPC or signing ran.

No commit, push, deploy, transmission or funds were used.
