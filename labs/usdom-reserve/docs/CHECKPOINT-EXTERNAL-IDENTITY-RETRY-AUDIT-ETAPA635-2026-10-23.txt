Stage 635 — checkpoint external identity and retry audit (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited only the Solidity checkpoint registry and the two RISC Zero adapters at
external ABI, returndata, revert and gas boundaries, with emphasis on accepted
candidates whose verifier or guardian identity changes before finalization.
The ABI-shape work from stage 616, domain binding from stage 618 and adapter
configuration work from stage 624 were treated as established and not repeated.

Result

No additional production-contract defect was confirmed.

- Verifier calls are static, gas-bounded and require exact returndata. A hostile
  verifier cannot mutate registry state, return a prefix plus trailing data or
  turn a revert/return bomb into acceptance.
- Submission checks the verifier identity both before and after proof
  verification. This closes configuration drift during the external call.
- Finalization rechecks the current verifier and guardian identities before
  advancing any checkpoint anchor. It intentionally does not repeat the
  expensive proof call: the selected statement and verifier identity were
  already fixed at admission.
- A temporary verifier configuration change or Safe guardian policy change
  causes a fail-closed revert without deleting or replacing the accepted
  candidate. The statement hash, original ready time, latest anchors and
  checkpoint count remain unchanged.
- Restoring both exact committed identities allows the same candidate to
  finalize after its original delay. The resulting predecessor progression
  makes submission of that checkpoint fail, so restoration does not open a
  replay path.
- Activation, chain, registry and configuration commitments remain part of the
  authenticated statement/configuration boundaries established in the earlier
  stages.

Material regression added

`testFinalizeExternalIdentityFailuresPreserveCandidateForExactRetry` selects an
authenticated checkpoint, reaches its ready time, then:

1. changes the verifier configuration and proves finalization fails while the
   candidate, ready time, anchors and count remain intact;
2. restores the verifier, adds a forbidden guardian Safe module and proves the
   second failure also preserves the same state;
3. restores the exact guardian policy and finalizes that original candidate;
   and
4. proves the finalized checkpoint cannot be submitted again.
