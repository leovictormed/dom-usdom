USD/DOM stage 430 — owner-bound production authorization
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

Stage 429 made the release and finalized preflight bind the exact ordered Safe
owner set. The governance EIP-1271 authorization digest still committed those
owners only indirectly through the release evidence and its residual-risk
report. A self-contained authorization should identify this high-authority
boundary directly.

Correction
==========

`ReleaseAuthorization` now includes `controlPlaneOwnersHash`, defined as the
Keccak-256 hash of canonical ABI encoding of the exact ordered three-address
array. Missing, duplicate or zero owners are rejected before a signing request
can be built. Any replacement or reordering changes the authorization digest.

The EIP-712 domain version is now `2`; signatures made for version 1 cannot be
presented as approvals of the expanded schema.
