ETAPA1046 — closed-mainnet deposit financial evidence

Concrete gap
------------
The pilot deposit executor proved three finalized transactions and allowance reset,
but its output-last record did not independently demonstrate the exact movement of
USDT into the reserve or the corresponding reserve accounting/deposit record.
Receipts alone were weaker than the real-funds result required by the pilot.

Correction
----------
Before any approval/deposit signature, the executor now observes on two RPCs at the
finalized tag: funder USDT balance, reserve USDT balance, totalDeposited and
outstanding. All observations must agree and are durably stored in a package-bound,
mode-0600 financial baseline.

After all receipts finalize, both RPCs must show: funder -1,000,000; reserve
+1,000,000; totalDeposited +1,000,000; outstanding +1,000,000; allowance zero; and
the package depositId mapped to the exact funder, amount and receiving commitment.
Output-last evidence binds the baseline SHA-256 and all before/after values. The
offline checklist independently validates the baseline and four exact deltas.
Unrelated balance/accounting movement during the ceremony fails closed.
