Stage 655 — Safe combined drift and owner returndata audit (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited the stage-651 checkpoint guardian owner commitment under simultaneous
owner/module/guard drift, restoration in different orders, malformed owner-list
ABI, revert and return-data bombs. Reviewed all production Safe authority
boundaries for fields checked only by shape and checked retryability after an
accepted candidate.

Result

No additional production-contract defect was confirmed.

- Registry and proof-reserve Safe identities bind proxy code, singleton address
  and code, exact ordered owner commitment, exact 2-of-3 threshold, empty module
  set, empty guard and the required mainnet fallback handler/code identity.
- Modules and guard are constrained to the single accepted empty state, so no
  additional identity exists to bind. Fallback is likewise an exact address and
  code hash on mainnet, rather than a shape-only check.
- Simultaneous drift cannot be repaired partially. Restoring the module while
  owners and guard remain changed still fails; restoring owners while guard
  remains changed also fails. Only the complete committed state reopens
  checkpoint actions.
- `getOwners()` is a gas-bounded static call into a fixed five-word output area.
  The decoder requires exactly 160 bytes, canonical offset/count, clean address
  words, distinct nonzero EOA owners and the immutable ordered commitment.
  Revert, 159-byte, 161-byte and one-megabyte responses fail closed without
  copying or allocating attacker-selected returndata.
- Finalize and permanent halt both fail before state mutation while identity is
  unhealthy. Candidate hash, original ready time, anchors and halt state remain
  unchanged, and exact restoration permits the original candidate to finalize.

Material regression

`testCombinedSafeDriftMalformedOwnersAndArbitraryRestoreRemainFailClosed`
combines owner, module and guard changes, restores them in a different order,
then exercises reverting, short, trailing and one-megabyte owner responses.
Every intermediate finalize/halt attempt fails, the candidate remains intact,
and complete restoration finalizes it.
