Stage 555 - RISC Zero incident response cancels an already scheduled resume

Scope
-----

Audited the production proof-reserve pause/recovery path, its upstream RISC Zero
health response planner and executor, plus the reserve's solvency and outflow
fail-closed checks. No deployment, transaction, signature, proof or funds action
was performed.

Finding and correction
----------------------

The reserve contract deliberately lets a generation-bound pause be submitted
again while the reserve is already paused. Besides preserving the pause, that
call clears `resumeReadyAt`. The incident-response planner did not use this
property: it omitted the reserve action whenever `paused == true`.

Consequently, governance could schedule a resume after the monitor had recorded
an unresolved RISC Zero health incident. Later monitor cycles saw the reserve
as paused and emitted no transaction, leaving the old resume authorization live.
After the governance delay, `executeResume` could reopen deposits and withdrawals
although the monitored upstream condition remained unhealthy.

The response schema is now v4 and binds `resumeReadyAt` in the authenticated
reserve snapshot. Every unhealthy classification emits the generation-bound
pause action when either the reserve is running or a resume is scheduled. The
action precondition binds queue epoch, pause state and exact resume timestamp;
the postcondition requires both `paused == true` and `resumeReadyAt == 0`.
Immediately before sending, the executor reobserves all three values. After
inclusion it independently proves that the pause remains active and the pending
resume was cancelled.

Other audited behavior
----------------------

- `USDOMProofReserve` rejects deposits and withdrawals while paused.
- Queue admission reserves collateral and rejects a reserve balance below the
  complete credited liability. Execution repeats solvency and exact USDT balance
  delta checks before accepting a payout.
- The token bucket is not reset by pause/resume and cannot exceed its immutable
  daily capacity.
- A pause invalidates the current pending queue in O(1), while paid burn and
  request identifiers remain permanent.
- Resume still requires the immutable governance delay and fresh token,
  verifier and control-plane checks.

Validation
----------

Two focused Node tests passed: one proves planning for an already paused reserve
with a scheduled resume, and one executes that plan through a mocked transaction
adapter and proves the resume timestamp becomes zero. The other nine lightweight
planner/executor tests also passed before the artifact-dependent isolated drill;
that drill could not start because its previously generated Foundry artifacts
had intentionally been cleaned. It is deferred until the active RandomX proof
releases resources and the normal Foundry build is run. No failure occurred in
the changed code path.

No readiness fact was changed. The fix prevents a fail-open recovery race; it
does not claim that an operational incident drill or production configuration
has already been completed.
