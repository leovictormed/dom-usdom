SAFE DEPLOYMENT BOUNDED RPC CHANNELS — STAGE 1180
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The three-Safe executor bounded RPC execution time but allowed Cast stdout and
stderr to flow directly into command substitutions and evidence files. A
malicious or defective RPC response could therefore cause unbounded shell
memory or disk growth; successful commands could also inject unexpected stderr
outside the authenticated evidence path.

Correction
==========

Every Cast RPC call now writes stdout and stderr to owner-only files in the
executor's tmpfs directory. Node verifies fatal UTF-8 decoding and strict limits
of 2 MiB for stdout and 64 KiB for stderr. Success requires empty stderr and
stdout is returned only through the pinned private-file reader. Failure emits
only a generic bounded status, without reflecting RPC-controlled content.
