ETAPA769 — OPERATIONAL BACKGROUND PROCESS INVENTORY
Date: 2026-10-23

Scope
-----
The mainnet deployment, Safe recovery and three Ledger signing shell ceremonies
were inventoried for background children, coprocesses, merged stdout/stderr,
ambiguous wait status and raw replay of child diagnostics. Proof, capacity and
local E2E harnesses were excluded from this operational boundary.

Result
------
No additional unsafe background process was found. The only background child in
scope is the private RPC catalog proxy in each executor. Both instances use the
already hardened protocol: pinned Node, distinct private stdout/stderr, bounded
fatal-UTF-8 validation, strict ready-file validation, captured PID, explicit
status handling, cleanup wait and fixed redacted failure diagnostics. The three
signing ceremonies launch no background child.

Preventive control
------------------
`operational-background-process-inventory.test.mjs` now fixes that inventory in
code. It rejects an extra background child, merged channels, coproc, raw proxy
diagnostic replay, and an ambiguous `wait ... || true` protocol. This makes a
future expansion of the process surface an explicit reviewed change.
