Stage 407 — Safe role owner separation

Finding
=======

The reserve previously validated the governance and guardian Safes independently,
but did not reject an owner appearing in both Safes. A shared owner weakens the
intended separation between ordinary administration and emergency control.

Correction
==========

USDOMProofReserve now requires disjoint governance and guardian owner sets when
both roles are Safe proxies. The constructor rejects overlap, and every value or
resume path continuously rechecks the relation through the same bounded Safe
owner reads used by controlPlaneHealthy(). Emergency pause remains callable even
when policy has drifted, preserving the fail-safe path.

The finalized-snapshot mainnet preflight independently rejects any governance
owner shared with either reserve guardian or checkpoint guardian. Sharing owners
between the two emergency roles remains permitted; neither can perform ordinary
reserve governance.
