USD0M stage 450 - reproducible dependency builds and live ratification collector

All three reserve dependencies now have source-derived reproducible-build
reports. The finalized-burn adapter, checkpoint adapter and checkpoint registry
each require two sequential Forge builds with isolated output/cache paths,
byte-identical artifacts, pinned compiler/source sets and SDK-derived runtime
and initcode hashes equal to the immutable release. The adapter reports also
recompute their exact verifier config; the registry report recomputes its full
checkpoint config. Inputs are reread after both builds.

The checkpoint-bootstrap ratification now has a live fail-closed collector. It
uses a create-once catalog of two to five operator IDs and origin commitments,
keeps URLs and the signature only in runtime environment, derives the exact
ratification digest, and requires every Ethereum mainnet RPC to agree on one
stable finalized snapshot, governance codehash and successful EIP-1271 result.
Strict bounded JSON-RPC rejects redirects, duplicate keys, oversized responses,
ID mismatches and snapshot drift.

Focused modules and integrated gate tests pass 19/19 for these paths. The real
isolated builds and live RPC ceremony remain release-time evidence and were not
executed. No commit, push, deployment, transaction, signature or funds occurred.
