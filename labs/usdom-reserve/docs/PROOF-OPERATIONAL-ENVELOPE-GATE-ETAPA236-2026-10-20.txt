Finding USDOM-236 (Medium)

The finalized-burn guest has a deterministic positive r0vm execution count,
but no cryptographic proof-generation latency or throughput measurement. The
checkpoint gate now requires proving-cost measurements for every mandatory
recursive layer, but neither Ethereum-facing path requires the gas consumed by
its exact receipt through the release-pinned adapter and verifier.

Guest execution cycles do not prove that a production prover can produce a
receipt within the operational deadline. Adapter unit tests with a mock
verifier do not prove that the real seal fits the release gas envelope. A
release could therefore satisfy implementation flags while remaining unable to
produce or verify proofs within practical bounds.

Correction

Both readiness modes now independently require:

- measured finalized-burn proving capacity for the release workload;
- measured EVM gas for the exact finalized-burn receipt through its adapter;
- measured EVM gas for the exact outer-checkpoint receipt through its adapter.

Each false value produces a stable independent blocker and is covered by a
mutation test. The fields intentionally remain false: no expensive proof run
is authorized, and synthetic estimates cannot replace measured receipts.

These requirements establish operational feasibility. They do not demand
zero risk or an external audit, and neither policy was added to the gate.
