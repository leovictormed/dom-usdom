ETAPA 1087 — SOLIDITY GAS / DOS BOUNDARY AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-26

Scope
-----
Adversarial review of loops, dynamic calldata, memory allocation, pagination,
pruning, candidate replacement, external-call gas, returndata bombs, proof size,
events and permissionless griefing in USDOMProofReserve,
DOMCheckpointRegistry, the two RISC Zero adapters and the legacy quorum reserve.
No deployment, broadcast, signing, funds, network access, commit or push.

Concrete finding and correction
-------------------------------
DOMCheckpointRegistry previously verified a complete Groth16 proof before
rejecting a candidate whose rank was already known to lose or whose immutable
challenge window had already closed. This did not corrupt state and the caller
paid the gas, but it made automation and honest relayers spend the full bounded
five-million-gas verifier allowance on a terminal local error.

`submitCheckpointCandidate` now calls a local
`_requireCandidateAdmissible(checkpoint, statementHash)` before the proof
adapter. `_selectCandidate` repeats the same check after verification, retaining
fail-closed protection if a future refactor introduces an intervening call.
The existing competition and closed-window regressions now deliberately use
unapproved proofs for the losing/late submissions; they can return the precise
local error only when the expensive verifier was skipped.

Other reviewed boundaries
-------------------------
* All proof entry points demand exactly 260 bytes and the canonical four-byte
  selector before encoding or forwarding proof calldata.
* Reserve queue pagination, head pruning and arbitrary stale-ID pruning are
  capped at 256. Pagination's duplicate scan is quadratic but strictly limited
  to 32,640 memory comparisons and its storage traversal is also capped.
* Quorum loops and signer-array events are capped at 16; signature count must
  equal the configured threshold before recovery begins.
* Prune/event batches may be chosen smaller by any caller. An oversized chosen
  batch can only revert that caller's transaction and cannot block independent
  single-entry cleanup.
* Every token, Safe, registry, adapter and upstream verifier interaction has a
  fixed gas allowance. Assembly copies zero, one, three or five words only and
  separately checks exact returndata size, so return bombs cannot force an
  attacker-sized copy or allocation.
* Candidate replacement requires a valid proof and strict deterministic rank,
  preserves the original readyAt, and is closed at readyAt. Replacements cannot
  extend finalization or use equal-rank arrival ordering to oscillate state.
* Events contain fixed-size burn/checkpoint structs or bounded signer arrays;
  proof bytes and arbitrary stale-ID arrays are never emitted.
