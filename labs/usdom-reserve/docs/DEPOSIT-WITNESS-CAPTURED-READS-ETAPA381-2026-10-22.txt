USDOM stage 381 — captured finalized-deposit witness inputs
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The finalized-deposit witness builder checked a source with symlink_metadata
and then called fs::read on the pathname. A replacement between those calls
could make the bytes differ from the file that passed the bounds/type check.

Correction
==========

The builder now opens one handle, compares the opened device, inode and length
with the direct named file, requires current-user ownership and one link, reads
through a maximum-plus-one bound, and verifies the captured length. All later
decoding and hashing use those captured bytes.

Validation
==========

The focused test accepts one exact source and rejects a symbolic link, a
multiply linked source and an over-bound source: 1/1 passed. Native USDOM v4
binary compilation completed as part of that test. Formatting passes.
