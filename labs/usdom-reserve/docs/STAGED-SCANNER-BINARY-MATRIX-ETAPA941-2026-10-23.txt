Compatibility correction
ETAPA940 treated every blob as UTF-8 and capped it at 8 MiB, which would reject
required public ELF/Wasm guests, receipts, seals and setup keys. The scanner is
now path-aware and scans the exact staged blob with two disjoint policies.

Exact accepted public binary classes
- *.elf with ELF magic; *.wasm with WebAssembly magic.
- Named program/guest/artifact/receipt/seal/image-id/proving-key/verifying-key
  binaries using .bin/.dat/.zkey/.receipt/.seal, bounded at 512 MiB.
Text/config remains fatal UTF-8 and bounded at 8 MiB. Private-input, witness,
credential, auth, password, seed and raw-transaction paths remain forbidden
regardless of bytes. Ambiguous non-UTF8 binary paths fail closed.

Fixture matrix: 4/4 tests pass (public formats, secret text, private/ambiguous
binary and invalid magic). Bash syntax and diff-check pass; no commit/push.
