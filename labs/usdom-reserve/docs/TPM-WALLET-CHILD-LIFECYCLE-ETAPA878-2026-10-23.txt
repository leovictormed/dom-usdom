ETAPA 878 — TPM AND RECEIVING-WALLET CHILD LIFECYCLE
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
-------
The four scoped wallet/TPM ceremonies had no background children and three had
status-preserving cleanup, but Cargo builds and credential-bearing binaries had
no outer deadline. The keystore provisioner also used one non-terminal trap for
EXIT/HUP/INT/TERM, so a caught signal could resume after deleting secrets.

Correction
----------
- Cargo builds now run in timeout-created process groups with a one-hour bound
  and 30-second TERM-to-KILL escalation.
- Wallet recovery create/verify, TPM keystore unlock/provision, recovery drill
  and TPM authority bootstrap now have five-minute bounds with five-second kill
  escalation.
- Keystore provisioning cleanup now clears traps, preserves the incoming status
  and exits 129/130/143 after deleting its secret directory.
- Existing executable/TPM tool pinning and the other three cleanup routines were
  preserved.

Validation
----------
- Bash syntax passed for all four scoped scripts.
- Lifecycle, TPM tool boundary, wallet recovery pinning and temporary cleanup
  tests passed 6/6 after reconciliation.
- Diff whitespace validation passed.
- No Cargo build, TPM access, password entry, RPC, signing or fund operation ran.
