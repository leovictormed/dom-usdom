ETAPA778 — EXECUTOR FLOCK AUTHORITY
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding and correction
----------------------
Both executors opened descriptor 9 on the evidence directory but acquired its
exclusive lock with PATH-resolved `flock`. Substitution could report success
without locking and allow two deployment or recovery ceremonies to mutate the
same append-only evidence namespace concurrently.

Each executor now pins `flock` by absolute path, owner, hardlink count, mode,
trusted parent directory, inode metadata and SHA-256. Its identity is rechecked
immediately after opening descriptor 9 and before acquisition through the pinned
path. Lock failure remains fail-closed with status 75.

Lifetime and trap audit
-----------------------
The structural regression proves exactly one `exec 9<...`, rejects descriptor
close/reuse, requires lock acquisition before the cleanup trap, and rejects any
literal PATH `flock`. The descriptor remains owned by the main shell until its
exit; cleanup runs while it remains open, so signal and normal cleanup cannot
release exclusion before temporary state cleanup finishes.

Validation
----------
Focused tests pass 15/15; both executor scripts pass `bash -n`; `git diff
--check` passes. No executor, RPC, signing, deployment or transmission ran.
