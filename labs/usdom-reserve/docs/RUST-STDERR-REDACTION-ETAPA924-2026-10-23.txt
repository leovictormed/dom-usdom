ETAPA924 — RUST STDERR REDACTION
Date: 2026-10-23

Scope and finding
- Audited the nine explicit production stderr sites from the ETAPA918 fixed
  inventory: one authenticated exporter and eight proof planner/prover/package
  entrypoints.
- All nine interpolated the complete internal error string. Their error chains
  include filesystem paths and parser/receipt/proof diagnostics, so a service
  manager or journal could persist private artifact topology and values quoted
  by lower-level decoders.

Correction
- All nine failure exits retain a stable operation-specific diagnostic and
  nonzero exit status but no longer interpolate the internal error chain.
- No proof, subprocess, build, secret, RPC or external operation was run.
- Focused structural test passes 1/1; rustfmt check and diff-check pass.

Residual boundary
- Rust entrypoints returning `Result` directly are a separate implicit stderr
  boundary and are not counted among the nine explicit ETAPA918 stderr sites.
  They require a separate inventory rather than silently broadening this one.
