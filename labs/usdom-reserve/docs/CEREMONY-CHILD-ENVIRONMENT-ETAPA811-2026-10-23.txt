ETAPA811 — CEREMONY CHILD ENVIRONMENT
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding and correction
----------------------
Deployment, Safe recovery and three Ledger/Cast signing ceremonies inherited
ambient loader, Node, Rust, Foundry, Cast and proxy controls. `LD_PRELOAD` or
`NODE_OPTIONS` could inject code into credential-bearing children; Foundry/Cast
variables could change wallet/RPC behavior; debug/proxy variables could expose
or redirect sensitive traffic.

All five flows now sanitize those classes before resolving or invoking tools.
They remove loader and dynamic-library hooks, Node injection/debug, TLS key logs,
Rust logging/wrappers/flags, all upper/lowercase proxy variables, and every
`FOUNDRY_*`/`CAST_*` variable. `HOME` remains available for Ledger integration,
and the flows continue to export only their explicitly validated USDOM inputs.

Validation
----------
A structural inventory covers all five flows. A local hostile-environment
harness proves every listed and prefix variable is absent after sanitization.
Focused pinning/environment tests pass 8/8; five scripts pass `bash -n`; `git
diff --check` passes. No Ledger, Cast, RPC, signing or deployment ran.
