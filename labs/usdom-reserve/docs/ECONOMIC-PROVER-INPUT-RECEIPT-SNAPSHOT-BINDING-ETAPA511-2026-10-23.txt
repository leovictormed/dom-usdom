USD0M stage 511 — economic prover input and receipt snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The economic prover reopened live plan/source indexes, private package inputs,
chunk and block transitions, witnesses, six guest artifacts and resumable
receipts throughout a long multi-job proving run. This allowed different jobs
or recursive block composition to observe different filesystem generations.

Correction
==========

The wrapper captures all three indexes, derives bounded canonical source and
receipt catalogs, captures every private source and existing resumable receipt,
and copies both pinned guest artifact triplets into one owner-only layout
replica. The complete proving run operates there. Verified chunk and block
receipts are durably installed before the final economic receipt-set index, so
an interrupted handoff cannot present a mixed receipt generation as complete.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook suite asserts isolated capture, both guest families,
durable receipt publication and index-last ordering. Readiness requires
`production_economic_prover_inputs_and_receipts_snapshotted=true`. This stage
does not execute proofs, access a network, sign, deploy or move funds.
