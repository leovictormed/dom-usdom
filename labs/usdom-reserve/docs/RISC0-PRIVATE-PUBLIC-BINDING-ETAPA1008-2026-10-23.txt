ETAPA1008 — RISC Zero burn/checkpoint private-input and public-journal binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Scope
- UDFINL04 finalized-burn guest and portable relation;
- complete checkpoint-segment and checkpoint-outer guests;
- recursive receipt assumptions, host receipt verification and Solidity burn statement.

Result
No concrete authorization gap was found. A permanent structural gate was added because this multi-layer invariant previously depended on dispersed tests and review.

Evidence
1. UDFINL04 rejects length mismatch/trailing bytes, canonically re-encodes the whole input, reexecutes its Ethereum checkpoint and native burn witnesses, and requires the recomputed FinalizedBurn ABI hash to equal the embedded statement hash.
2. The burn guest verifies the exact pinned checkpoint-outer ImageID and exact CheckpointStatementV4 hash before committing exactly the 32-byte finalized-burn statement hash.
3. Each segment transition commits the domain-separated canonical bytes of all private block/package/native/economic witnesses. Execution checks that commitment and block count, derives all subordinate receipt journals, and commits only the 32-byte transition commitment.
4. The outer relation validates exact policy/opening/segment continuity and pinned segment ImageID, then commits only the 32-byte CheckpointStatementV4 ABI hash.
5. FinalizedBurn's Rust type string and ABI word construction include burnId, requestId, recipient, amount, DOM domain/height/block/state root, Ethereum-finality slot, deadline, chain ID, reserve and activation schedule. USDOMProofReserve recomputes this statement from calldata and passes it to the verifier, preventing reuse for another burn or deployment.
6. Journals expose only 32-byte commitments. They do not commit raw note history, nullifiers beyond the public burn identity, deposit lineage, openings, policy bytes or witness bytes. Host-side SHA-256 values authenticate local/private proof-job artifacts; they are not guest journal fields or Solidity inputs.
