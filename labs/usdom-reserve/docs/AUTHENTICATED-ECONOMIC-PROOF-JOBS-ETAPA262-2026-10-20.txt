Outcome

Authenticated inputs

1. The separate economic source index must bind the exact SHA-256 of the
   canonical checkpoint source-set index.
2. Every segment and block index must be consecutive and cover the complete
   previous-height through confirmation-height interval.
3. Every segment private input is read as a direct regular file, size bounded,
   hash checked and canonically decoded. The package selected for each job is
   therefore the exact package later consumed by the segment relation.
4. Every economic block claim must equal the exact recursive economic bytes in
   that segment block.
5. Every chunk transition and witness is a direct bounded regular file with its
   exact canonical name and index hash.
6. The planner reexecutes every chunk over the selected package, checks the
   resulting transition byte-for-byte, composes the ordered chain, checks the
   terminal block claim and enforces root continuity between blocks.
7. Both economic guest ELF, ProgramBinary and ImageID triplets are pinned by
   fixed paths and reviewed SHA-256/ImageID values.

`generates_proofs=false` and `mines=false`.
