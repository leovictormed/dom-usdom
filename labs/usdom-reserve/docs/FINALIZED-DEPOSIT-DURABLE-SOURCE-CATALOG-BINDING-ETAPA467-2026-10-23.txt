Stage 467 — finalized-deposit durable source/catalog binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The finalized-deposit collector validated and reconciled multiple execution
and Beacon sources against a catalog, but the durable source document discarded
the catalog identities after collection. The resulting proof input could not
show which independent endpoint origins produced the reconciled observation.

Remediation
===========

The durable finalized-deposit source now embeds the exact canonical source
catalog, its source count and a domain-separated commitment over the ordered
operator, execution-origin and Beacon-origin commitments. Validation checks the
catalog schema and independence, recomputes the commitment and requires the
exact count. Catalog or commitment substitution fails closed.

Evidence boundary
=================

`production_finalized_deposit_durable_source_binds_catalog` is a structural
fact. It does not claim a live finalized deposit was observed. No network,
signing, deployment, funds, mining, commit or push occurred.

- Changed Node syntax, JSON and diff checks pass.
