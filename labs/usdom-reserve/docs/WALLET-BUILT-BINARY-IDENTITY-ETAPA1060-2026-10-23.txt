ETAPA1060 — WALLET BUILT-BINARY IDENTITY
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
The recovery workflows authenticated Cargo but then trusted executable paths under
target/release. A preexisting symlink or concurrent replacement between build and
execution could redirect a credential-bearing operation to an unauthenticated file.

Correction
==========
- Every generated wallet/recovery executable must be a direct regular executable,
  non-symlink, single-link, owner-controlled and not group/world writable.
- Device, inode, size, timestamps, owner, mode, link count and SHA-256 are captured
  before password entry and rechecked immediately before each execution.
- Receiving-wallet, TPM-keystore and checkpoint-recovery binaries are all covered.

Evidence
========
Explicit Node v24.19.0 wallet boundary suites passed 5/5.
Gate: python3 labs/usdom-reserve/scripts/check-wallet-built-binary-identity-stage1060.py
Broad mainnet readiness remained green: 62/62 passed.

Scope
=====
No Cargo build, network, deployment, signing, push, secrets or funds.
