ETAPA 814 — credential-flow descriptor isolation
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
The six wallet/TPM/burn/deposit-witness entrypoints inherited caller-owned file
descriptors. Five interactive flows also left their terminal stdin attached to
credential-bearing children after password entry. A caller's evidence, lock or
log descriptor, or unread terminal input, could therefore cross an unrelated
child boundary.

Correction
==========
Each entrypoint now closes inherited descriptors above 2 before resolving or
starting any child. Bash's internal script descriptor 255 is retained in the
shell and is CLOEXEC. Interactive flows revoke stdin with /dev/null immediately
after the last password/TPM authorization read; their pre-password Cargo build
also receives /dev/null. The noninteractive witness builder revokes stdin at
entry. Stdout and stderr remain the operator terminal; no credential is copied
to a log sink, and credentials continue to move through owner-only files.

Validation
==========
- Local /proc/fd harness injects fd 9 plus unread stdin and proves neither
  reaches the child.
- Descriptor/environment/cleanup tests: 16/16 pass.
- bash -n: all six changed entrypoints pass.
- git diff --check: pass.

No TPM, RPC, Cargo build, signature, deployment, transmission or fund operation
was executed.
