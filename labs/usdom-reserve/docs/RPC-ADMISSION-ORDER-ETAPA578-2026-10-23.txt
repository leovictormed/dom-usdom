RPC expensive-work admission order — stage 578
Date: 2026-10-23
Scope: crates/dom-rpc/src/lib.rs

Finding corrected
=================
The candidate-V2, finalized-issuance, private transfer/burn and isolated
Mainnet mining routers attached their per-source governor directly to each
method and then wrapped the route in a concurrency limiter. Tower evaluates
the later outer layer first, so a request could wait for the single expensive
work permit before reaching the rate limit. Excess authenticated traffic could
therefore accumulate waiting service futures for the ordinary 30-second,
issuance 120-second, or mining four-hour timeout window instead of receiving an
immediate 429.

Correction
==========
Each expensive route group now wraps its concurrency/body/timeout stack with
the governor. After bearer authentication, per-source rate admission therefore
happens before waiting for the serialized proof or mining permit. Candidate-V2,
issuance and isolated mining retain their prior independent buckets. Private
transfer and private burn share both the same proof-verifying runtime and one
submit bucket, preventing a caller from multiplying admission capacity by
alternating the two route names.

Body limits and timeouts remain outside or alongside the work queue as
appropriate: queued bodies are not decoded by handlers, every ordinary wait is
bounded by 30 seconds, issuance by 120 seconds and isolated mining by four
hours. The change only rejects over-quota work sooner; it does not grant any
new capability or change consensus validation.
