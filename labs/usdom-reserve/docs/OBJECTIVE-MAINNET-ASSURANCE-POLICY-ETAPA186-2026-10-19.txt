Stage 186 - objective mainnet assurance and residual-risk policy
Date: 2026-10-19

Operator direction

The operator explicitly directed that absence of an external audit and absence
of an impossible zero-risk guarantee must not, by themselves, block mainnet.
This changes release policy only. It does not authorize deployment, real funds,
pushes or the conversion of unfinished technical work into a passing fact.

Gate changes

1. The release gate no longer requires external DOM-consensus, privacy-circuit
   or Solidity audit reports. Optional reports remain hash-validated and cannot
   be presented with malformed or mismatched evidence.
2. Four review-only requirement flags were removed from the hard requirement
   list: independent checkpoint-registry audit, independent issuance review,
   independent privacy-circuit audit and independent control-plane review.
3. Two objective documents are now mandatory: securityValidationReport and
   residualRiskAcceptanceReport. They must be distinct, present and addressed
   by exact SHA-256 like the other release evidence.
4. The operator-intent and production-requirement records now state that neither
   an external audit nor absolute zero risk is required. Release still requires
   passing technical validation, reproducible artifacts, adversarial tests,
   operational drills and explicit acceptance of documented residual risk.

Regression protection

A release-fixture test sets every external-audit/review flag false, supplies no
audit reports and sets an absolute-zero-risk flag false. Both closed-mainnet and
real-funds assessments still pass when every objective requirement and evidence
item is valid. Separate mutations prove that a missing security-validation or
risk-acceptance report and malformed optional audit evidence fail closed.

Current status

This policy removes only assurance conditions that the operator rejected. It
does not remove unfinished proof execution, absent production configuration,
missing operational identities, failed or absent vectors, or explicit deploy
and real-funds authorization. Those are factual blockers and remain enforced.
