ORDER OF ACTIONS

3. If interruption occurred after approve(1 USDT) but before deposit, the funder

4. Do not issue USDOM unless a finalized reserve deposit and its authenticated

5. Inventory paid flags, proved debt and queue epoch before recovery.

PHASE MATRIX
- before approve: pause + close; verify zero allowance and no debt.
- after approve: pause + close; funder revokes allowance to zero; no issuance.
- after deposit: pause + close; collateral remains reserved; no refund/sweep.
- after issuance: pause + close; preserve backing and stop new activity.
- after burn: pause + close; permanent proved debt is retained. After controlled
  governance resume, renew only that existing debt lease and pay it.
- after queue: pause invalidates the lease but not the debt. Resume after delay,
  renew exact existing debt, wait the withdrawal delay and pay it.
- after payout: pause + close; verify paidBurns/paidRequests and conservation.

IMPLEMENTED BOUNDARY
Permanent closure now rejects a first proof that would create a new debt while
allowing renewal of an already proved debt. Thus closeDeposits stops both new
collateral and new first-proof queue admission, while existing unpaid burn debt
remains renewable/payable after a controlled resume.

No collateral sweep or deposit refund exists. Neither is part of recovery.
Pausing temporarily blocks payout as well as queue/deposit; therefore permanent
closure plus delayed controlled resume is required to finish existing debts.
