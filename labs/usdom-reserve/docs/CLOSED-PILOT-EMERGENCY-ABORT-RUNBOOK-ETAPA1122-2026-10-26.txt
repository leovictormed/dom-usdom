CLOSED PILOT EMERGENCY ABORT — ETAPA 1122
Date: 2026-10-26

ORDER OF ACTIONS
1. If authorizedPilotDeposit is nonzero and pilotDepositConsumed is false,
   governance or guardian Safe executes
   cancelPilotDepositAuthorization(authorizedPilotSnapshot). A stale snapshot
   must revert. Confirm authorizedPilotDeposit, authorizedPilotSnapshot and
   authorizedPilotValidUntil are zero on every catalog RPC. Expiry prevents a
   deposit but does not replace this state cleanup before a new generation.
2. Guardian Safe executes pause() or the observed-generation-bound
   pauseQueueEpoch(queueEpoch). Confirm paused=true and the new empty queue epoch
   on every catalog RPC.
3. Guardian Safe executes closeDeposits(). This closure is irreversible. Confirm
   depositsClosed=true on every RPC before handling allowances.
4. If interruption occurred after approve(1 USDT) but before deposit, the funder
   Ledger sends approve(reserve,0). The Safe cannot revoke another account's
   allowance. Confirm allowance=0 on every RPC.
5. Do not issue USDOM unless a finalized reserve deposit and its authenticated
   source already exist. Stop native issuance/burn workflows at their durable
   phase boundary.
6. Inventory paid flags, proved debt and queue epoch before recovery.

PHASE MATRIX
- after EIP-1271 but before gate: archive an expired generation automatically;
  never delete request/signature/state manually.
- after gate but before approve: cancel the exact snapshot on-chain, then pause
  + close; preserve gate plan/signatures/package/raw/receipt/completion.
- before approve: pause + close; verify zero allowance and no debt.
- after approve: pause + close; funder revokes allowance to zero; no issuance.
- after deposit: pause + close; collateral remains reserved; no refund/sweep.
- after issuance: pause + close; preserve backing and stop new issuance. After
  controlled recovery, already-issued supply may still transfer or burn under
  DOM consensus so every holder retains a path to redemption.
- after burn: pause + close; after controlled governance resume, submit the
  first finalized-burn proof if it was not yet accepted, or renew its exact
  existing debt lease, then pay it.
- after queue: pause invalidates the lease but not the debt. Resume after delay,
  renew exact existing debt, wait the withdrawal delay and pay it.
- after payout: pause + close; verify paidBurns/paidRequests and conservation.

IMPLEMENTED BOUNDARY
Permanent closure rejects new collateral deposits and provides the authenticated
issuance ceiling. It does not reject the first proof of a finalized burn: doing
so would strand USDOM issued before closure and make the required supply-zero
shutdown unreachable. First proofs remain limited to unreserved credited
collateral and must pass the immutable finalized-burn verifier; existing unpaid
burn debt remains renewable and payable after a controlled resume.

No collateral sweep or deposit refund exists. Neither is part of recovery.
Pausing temporarily blocks payout as well as queue/deposit; therefore permanent
closure plus delayed controlled resume is required to finish existing debts.
