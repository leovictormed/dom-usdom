ETAPA 1020 — executable release-authorization regression
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
The ETAPA1007 structural test still required the pre-ETAPA1011 inline expression
keccak256(toUtf8Bytes(canonicalJson(releaseEvidence))). ETAPA1011 intentionally
split canonicalization, the 4 MiB bound, and hashing into three statements. The
implementation was correct, but the stale structural assertion failed under the
pinned Node runtime and could no longer serve as a release gate.

Correction
The gate now independently requires:
1. canonicalJson(releaseEvidence) assigned to encoded;
2. the 4 MiB UTF-8 byte-length bound applied to encoded; and
3. keccak256(toUtf8Bytes(encoded)) returned.
This preserves the security property and makes removal of the size bound or
hashing step independently detectable.
