ETAPA740 — RPC numeric authority canonicalization
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding and correction
======================

The prior deployment transaction nonce was converted by an inline unconstrained
`BigInt`, and several RPC decimal/hex quantities reached Bash comparisons after
only permissive regex checks. Although downstream policy bounds covered common
values, noncanonical encodings and extreme values were not rejected uniformly.

The recovery nonce now uses the canonical Ethereum-quantity uint64 helper before
comparison with the planned decimal nonce. The executor inventory also migrated
RPC gas estimates, block gas limits, base fees, balances and persisted maximum
cost through shared canonical parsers. Hex quantities are lowercase canonical
and uint64-bounded. Decimal quantities reject signs, leading zeros, exponent or
fraction syntax and are BigInt-bounded to uint256; narrower operational bounds
are applied before any Bash arithmetic. No JS Number conversion is used.

Only local loop indexes, seconds and already bounded small policy values remain
in Bash arithmetic. Financial balances and costs remain in BigInt checks.

Verification
============

- Canonical zero, uint64 max and uint256 max pass.
- Overflow, leading zero, sign, exponent, fraction, malformed hex/decimal and
  reversed-bound cases fail.
- Focused Node tests: 15/15 passed.
- Bash syntax and `git diff --check`: passed.

No real RPC, signature, deployment, funds action, commit or push occurred.
