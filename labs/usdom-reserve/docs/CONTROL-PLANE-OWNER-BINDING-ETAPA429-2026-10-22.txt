USD/DOM stage 429 — release-bound Safe owners
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The semantic Safe recovery evidence authenticated the exact ordered owner set,
but the last finalized-snapshot mainnet preflight accepted any three distinct
EOAs with threshold two. An owner rotation after evidence approval could retain
that generic shape and cross the final preflight without matching the release.

Correction
==========

The exact release now contains `controlPlaneOwners`. The Safe materializer
derives it from the three mutually consistent post-recovery role reports. The
readiness gate reconciles those reports with the release, and the preflight
requires the live ordered owners of governance, reserve guardian and checkpoint
guardian to equal the release. Missing, duplicate, reordered or substituted
owners fail closed.

Recovery remains supported. A legitimate owner replacement produces new
semantic evidence and a newly approved release before subsequent authorized
use; it is not silently accepted under the prior release.
