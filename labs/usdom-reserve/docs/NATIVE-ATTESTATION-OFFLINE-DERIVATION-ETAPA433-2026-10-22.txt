USD/DOM stage 433 — independent native-attestation derivation at signing
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The two-owner Ledger workflow extracted four stored digests from the native
attestation request and signed them directly. The final materializer later
rebuilt each request against the release, but hardware approval had already
occurred. The signing boundary did not independently prove that the privacy,
runtime, custody and recovery subjects came from the current authoritative
artifacts.

Correction
==========

A fixed offline helper now:

- authenticates the exact release, release evidence and SHA-256-addressed Safe
  bundle, including the three ordered owners;
- reruns the locked Rust `dom-usdom-manifest-check` for mainnet;
- hashes the exact native runtime manifest and wallet public identity;
- reconciles that identity with TPM custody and the completed wallet recovery
  drill;
- rebuilds all four attestations, typed-data payloads and EIP-712 digests;
- rejects malformed finalized anchors, subject substitution, digest changes,
  unsafe HOME identity and any artifact change during validation.

The helper runs immediately before Ledger signing and again after both Ledger
interactions. The final native EIP-1271 materializer repeats it before preparing
the finalized observation and before publishing outputs, while retaining byte
snapshots of the request, release, Safe bundle and signatures.
