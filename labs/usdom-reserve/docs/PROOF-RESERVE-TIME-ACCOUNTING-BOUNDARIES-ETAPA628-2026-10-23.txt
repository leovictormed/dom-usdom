Stage 628 — proof-reserve time and accounting boundaries (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited only Solidity arithmetic and temporal boundaries in
`USDOMProofReserve`: uint64 timestamp/amount conversions, finality-slot age,
queue readiness/expiry, token-bucket refill, maximum exposure, outstanding and
unreserved collateral, donations, insolvency and hostile USDT policy changes.

Result

No additional contract defect was confirmed.

- Constructor and scheduled timestamps use checked uint64 narrowing. Queue and
  resume scheduling revert before mutation if `block.timestamp + delay` cannot
  fit. Execution also checks the narrowed bucket timestamp before value moves.
- Amounts entering deposits and burns are uint64, while cumulative deposited,
  released and pending values are uint256. `outstanding` is capped by the
  uint64 `maxOutstanding`; pending admission cannot exceed unreserved credited
  collateral. Releasing a pending amount decrements the reservation in the
  same reverting transaction that increments released credit.
- Donations increase token balance without increasing credited collateral.
  Confiscation below outstanding credit causes both admission and execution to
  fail insolvent. Exact token balance deltas reject fee/rebase behavior.
- Paused, deprecated, fee-enabled or blacklisted token states fail before
  consuming a new proof; changes after queue leave the reservation and bucket
  intact so the exact debt can retry after policy recovery.
- Finality admission explicitly rejects timestamps before Ethereum genesis,
  future slots and ages greater than seven days. The maximum allowed age is
  inclusive. Slot zero remains an invalid sentinel; slot one is accepted at
  the first corresponding post-genesis timestamp.
- Queue readiness and expiry are inclusive at their exact timestamp:
  execution is allowed when `timestamp == readyAt == expiresAt` as applicable,
  while discard/prune begins only after expiry. This matches the paired `>` and
  `<=` predicates and leaves no gap or double-valid cleanup instant.
- Bucket refill uses uint256 arithmetic, returns capacity directly after one
  day, and caps the fractional path. Since stored balance and capacity are
  uint64 and elapsed is below one day on that path, multiplication cannot
  approach uint256 overflow. An execution stores only `available - amount`.

Material regressions added

1. `testFinalityExpiryAndBucketBoundaryEqualitiesRemainExecutable` admits a
   burn at the exact maximum finality age, executes it at the exact queue
   expiry, checks fractional refill at `24h - 1`, checks full capacity at
   exactly `24h`, executes again and reconciles released/outstanding credit.
2. `testBeaconGenesisAndExactFirstNonzeroSlotFailClosedWithoutUnderflow`
   verifies a pre-genesis timestamp rejects with the contract error rather than
   arithmetic underflow, then admits slot one at exactly genesis plus 12
   seconds without consuming state during the failed attempt.
