ETAPA971 — NATIVE USDOM PUBLIC AUTHORITY MODE POLICY
Date: 2026-10-23

Threat model: manifests, verifying keys, restart/checkpoint witnesses and
activation/sync policy are not secrets and may be world-readable. They are
startup/consensus authority, so neither their leaf inode nor the immediate name
authority directory may be writable by group or other. Both must be owned by
the effective node user. Descriptor retention, no-follow, nlink=1 and inode
rechecks still protect the capture window.

The shared public `read_captured_bytes` already enforced owner, regular type,
single link, stable device/inode/name and bounded reads, but omitted mode bits.
An owner-created 0664 artifact or 0770 parent could therefore be mutated by a
group member without violating those checks. Initial and final audits now
reject `mode & 0o022 != 0` for named/retained parent and named/anchored/retained
leaf. Mode 0644 remains accepted; secrecy is intentionally not required.

Rust regressions cover accepted 0644, rejected 0664 and rejected 0770 parent.
Focused structural gate 2/2, rustfmt and diff-check passed. Cargo was deferred
while the proof runs. No external operation was executed.
