Stage 488 — Safe recovery target envelope-role binding
Date: 2026-10-23

The Safe recovery target preflight previously accepted its role selector
through the environment even though the canonical recovery envelope already
commits the exact role and transaction semantics. A caller could therefore
request an observation under semantics supplied separately from the durable
ceremony artifacts.

The preflight now directly reads the recovery plan, transaction package and
envelope, semantically validates their relationship, and derives the role
exclusively from the envelope. After independent cataloged multi-RPC
observation, it rereads those three inputs together with the Safe package and
RPC catalog before emitting a result. Substitution, omission, a loose role and
TOCTOU changes fail closed.

`production_safe_recovery_target_role_derived_from_envelope` is a structural
fact. No network access, signature, transaction, funds, mining, commit or push
occurred while producing this evidence.

Validation: direct operational inputs and complete operator runbook 84/84;
readiness 60/60.
