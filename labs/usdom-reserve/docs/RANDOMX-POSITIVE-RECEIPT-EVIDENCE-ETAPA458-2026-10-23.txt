Stage 458 — RandomX positive-receipt evidence boundary
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The Rust proof host verifies each succinct RandomX receipt against the pinned
per-block ImageID and exact UDCRXST1 commitment before publishing its receipt
index. The capacity collector later checked index schemas and artifact hashes,
but trusted boolean execution claims in that index. It did not bind the bytes
it collected to the proof host's post-verification summary. Replacement of an
index and its receipt files between proving and collection could therefore
produce internally consistent but non-cryptographic RandomX layer evidence.
The recursively verified outer proof would remain valid, so this was an
evidence-integrity flaw rather than a consensus bypass.

Correction

The capacity executor now captures and strictly parses the proof host's exact,
bounded terminal summary. The accepted grammar requires proved-and-verified,
one to 720 receipts, nonzero job and receipt-index SHA-256 values, succinct
receipts, development mode disabled and no mining. Extra, duplicate or altered
lines are rejected.

The collector now returns the exact job-index hash, receipt-index hash and
receipt count observed with the receipt graph. It also includes the receipt
index itself in the RandomX capacity input-set commitment. Before any later
proof layer is credited to the report, the executor requires those three
observations to equal the cryptographic proof-host summary. Release inputs are
rechecked immediately before report construction and again after durable
publication.

Verification

- summary/collector/capacity focused tests: 12/12 passed;
- mutations cover status, count, both hashes, receipt kind, development mode,
  mining, trailing data and post-verification graph substitutions;
- JavaScript syntax, production requirements JSON and diff checks pass.

Status

The structural evidence boundary is complete and recorded by
`production_checkpoint_randomx_verified_summary_bound_to_capacity_evidence`.
`production_checkpoint_randomx_complete_guest_positive_receipt_executed`
remains false until the final release-bound guest actually executes and its
