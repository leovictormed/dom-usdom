Finding USDOM-234 (High)

The production requirements ledger already distinguished three boundaries:
the portable finalized-burn relation, successful execution of the RISC Zero
guest and acceptance of a non-development receipt by the release-pinned EVM
verifier. The third field,
production_finalized_burn_risc0_evm_receipt_verifier_implemented, correctly
remained false because no receipt has been generated or verified.

The readiness gate required the broad
production_finalized_burn_verifier_implemented flag and the guest execution
and benchmark fields, but omitted the existing EVM receipt-verification field.
A release author could therefore mark only the broad flag true and allow a
successful executor run to stand in for cryptographic proof verification.

Correction

Both closed-mainnet and real-funds readiness now require
production_finalized_burn_risc0_evm_receipt_verifier_implemented explicitly.
The complete ready fixture supplies it, and a dedicated mutation test proves
that changing only this field to false produces its exact unique blocker.

Release boundary

This condition may become true only after the complete raw finalized-burn
witness produces a non-development RISC Zero receipt and that receipt is
accepted by the release-pinned verifier bytecode under the exact ImageID and
journal. Stage 233 executed the guest and measured cycles; it did not generate
a proof and therefore does not satisfy this boundary.

Independent external review may strengthen confidence but remains optional.
An absolute zero-risk guarantee is not required. Receipt generation and exact
verifier acceptance are objective technical evidence and cannot be replaced by
residual-risk acceptance.
