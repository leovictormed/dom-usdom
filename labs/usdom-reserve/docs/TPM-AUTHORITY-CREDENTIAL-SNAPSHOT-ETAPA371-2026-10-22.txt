USDOM stage 371 — TPM authority credential snapshot binding

credential once into a bounded private buffer. Authorization restoration and

no-follow, no-clobber private writer. Newly published evidence is reread and
compared byte-for-byte, while an existing destination is accepted only when it
already contains the exact canonical evidence.
