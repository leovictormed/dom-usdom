Stage 464 — finalized-burn release EVM receipt binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The release-specific finalized-burn E2E report stored EVM acceptance and gas
without committing the isolated Anvil transaction receipt. Consequently, its
transaction/block provenance could be replaced independently of the derived
result.

Remediation
===========

E2E report schema v4 requires a canonical
`usdom-finalized-burn-release-evm-receipt-v1` transcript. It binds transaction
hash, block hash and block number to the release adapter, its runtime and
configuration hashes, the finalized-burn statement, EVM-seal SHA-256 and gas.
The release executor creates it directly from the successful receipt and exact
verified call inputs, writes the exact transcript bytes and reports their hash.
Semantic validation independently reconstructs the transcript, requires hash
uniqueness, and rejects legacy schema and every tested substitution.

Evidence boundary
=================

`production_finalized_burn_release_evm_receipt_bound_to_e2e_report` and the v4
schema enforcement are structural facts. They do not claim a live release run.
No network, signing, deployment, funds, mining, commit or push occurred.
