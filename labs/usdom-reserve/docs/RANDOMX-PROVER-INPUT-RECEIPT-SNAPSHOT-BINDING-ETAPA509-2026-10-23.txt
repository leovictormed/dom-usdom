USD0M stage 509 — RandomX prover input and receipt snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The long-running RandomX prover reopened its live job index, statement index,
statements, guest artifacts and resumable receipts only after Cargo startup.
Those files could change between command authorization and an individual proof
job, and a live receipt cache could be replaced while later jobs ran.

Correction
==========

The wrapper now captures both indexes, validates bounded unique statement and
receipt catalogs, captures every statement and existing resumable receipt, and
copies the SHA-pinned ELF/program/ImageID into an owner-only layout replica.
The prover operates only on that replica for its entire run. Verified receipts
are captured and durably installed before the final receipt-set index. Thus an
interrupted handoff remains fail-closed under the previous index hashes; the new
index becomes visible only after every referenced receipt is durable.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook suite asserts private capture, isolated prover root,
durable receipt publication and index-last ordering. Readiness requires
`production_randomx_prover_inputs_and_receipts_snapshotted=true`. Tests do not
execute proofs, access a network, sign, deploy or move funds.
