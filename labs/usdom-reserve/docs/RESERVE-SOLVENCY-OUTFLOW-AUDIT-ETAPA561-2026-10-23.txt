USD + DOM — reserve solvency and outflow audit
Stage 561 — 2026-10-23

Scope
-----

Audited `USDOMProofReserve` deposit, queue, execution, queue invalidation,
cleanup, pause/resume and token-bucket accounting for a path that could pay
while insolvent, exceed the configured outflow capacity, restore spent capacity
through an administrative transition or leave collateral double-reserved.

Result
------

No distinct exploitable defect was established.

- Deposits measure exact reserve and funder balance deltas. They reject unsafe
  USDT policy, a changed token runtime, existing insolvency and exposure above
  the immutable maximum before creating credit.
- Queue admission checks unreserved credit and complete reserve solvency before
  the expensive proof, then reserves the exact amount under one queue epoch.
- Execution rechecks the exact statement, epoch, delay, lease, verifier, token
  policy, credited liability, live reserve balance and rate limit. State and
  replay identifiers update before the non-reentrant transfer, and any transfer
  or exact-delta failure reverts the complete transaction.
- The bucket is capped at the immutable capacity and refills conservatively over
  24 hours. Pause, resume and queue invalidation do not reset either bucket
  field, so administrative activity cannot manufacture withdrawal capacity.
- Emergency pause advances `queueEpoch` once and clears only current reservation
  aggregates. Old entries cannot decrement the new queue, overwrite an active
  request or clear a replacement request during lazy cleanup.
- Paid burn and request identifiers have no deletion path. Donations do not
  create credit, and `outstanding = totalDeposited - totalReleased` cannot be
  increased by a direct token transfer.

The contract therefore fails closed if collateral is externally confiscated,
the reserve is blacklisted, token policy changes, the verifier/control plane
changes or capacity is exhausted. Separate operational alerts remain useful for
response time, but their absence does not authorize a payout that these checks
reject.

Validation and limits
---------------------

Four lightweight cross-language statement and burn-identity tests passed. The
complete Solidity and invariant campaigns are queued after the active RandomX
proof because cleaned Foundry artifacts would require the deliberately deferred
heavy rebuild. No code or readiness fact changed in this audit. No external
RPC, proof, signing, deployment, transaction, funds, commit or push operation
ran.
