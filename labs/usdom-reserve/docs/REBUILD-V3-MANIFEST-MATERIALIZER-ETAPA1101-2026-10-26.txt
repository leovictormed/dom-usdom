ETAPA 1101 — REBUILD V3 MANIFEST MATERIALIZER
Date: 2026-10-26

Added a create-once V3 manifest materializer driven only by a SHA-256 pinned
local recipe. It requires the complete eight-stage graph and non-placeholder
producer commands, refuses absent real receipt/proof evidence, resolves the
verify-rebuild-receipt and aggregator executables to direct executable regular
files, recomputes their hashes and compares separate reviewed pins.

For every declared receipt it derives the full verify-only argv and output,
then derives the aggregator argv from the authenticated receipts-stage input.
Both executable paths/hashes are inserted into the manifest tool inventory.
The result is written mode 0600 with O_EXCL/O_NOFOLLOW and file/directory fsync.

Lightweight tests pass 4/4: complete recipe materialization, missing real
receipts, substituted executable hash, and verifier binary not built. Python
compilation and diff checks pass.

The real verify-rebuild-receipt binary is currently absent from both known
target/debug and target/release paths, and real receipt/proof evidence is not
available. Consequently no production manifest was materialized. No Cargo,
Forge, network, deployment, funds, commit or push was used.
