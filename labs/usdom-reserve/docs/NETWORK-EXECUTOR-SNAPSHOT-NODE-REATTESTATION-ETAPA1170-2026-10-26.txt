NETWORK EXECUTOR SNAPSHOT NODE REATTESTATION — STAGE 1170
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The contract-deployment and Safe-recovery executors pinned Node at startup, but
their reusable private snapshot functions invoked that saved pathname without
immediately reattesting its inode, parent directory and SHA-256. This weakened
the final artifact barriers added in stages 1160 and 1161: the private reader
could be entered through a runtime that was no longer the authenticated one.

Correction
==========

Both snapshot functions now call their complete `assert_node_unchanged`
predicate immediately before every private-reader invocation. Because the
check lives in the shared function, it covers startup snapshots, resume state,
installed artifact rereads and the final success barriers, including future
call sites.
