Stage 473 — Safe recovery envelope RPC-consensus binding
Date: 2026-10-23

The Safe recovery envelope planner received catalog-matched RPC sources but
reduced their identities to rpc1/rpc2 labels in the durable envelope. The
planner now accepts the matched source objects, validates their exact canonical
operator/origin set and stores domain-separated RPC-consensus evidence inside
the envelope hash.

Envelope validation recomputes that evidence and requires its source count to
equal the number of persisted observations. Source substitution, omission,
duplication and commitment changes therefore fail closed even when an attacker
recomputes the outer envelope hash. The caller still rereads the catalog and
plan inputs before publishing the envelope.

`production_safe_recovery_envelope_binds_rpc_consensus` is structural. No live
recovery, network, signature, deployment, funds, mining, commit or push is
claimed.

All legacy Safe-control fixtures were migrated to the durable RPC-consensus
binding used by production collectors. Validation: Safe-control module 15/15,
direct-input dependents 13/13, complete operator runbook 71/71 with the explicit
operational PATH, and readiness 60/60.
