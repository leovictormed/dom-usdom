USD0M stage 528 — checkpoint capacity materializer run-snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The checkpoint capacity release-evidence materializer read the succeeded status
and capacity report directly from the selected run. Although it reread both
later, parsing and evidence preparation had no owner-only immutable replica and
the initial concurrent reads could span different filesystem generations.

Correction
==========

The selected completion pair is now captured into a fresh owner-only temporary
replica. Both installed files are reread and checked for exact byte and SHA-256
equality. Parsing and evidence preparation consume only replica bytes. Both
origins are revalidated by bytes and hashes before immutable report publication
and again before the release-evidence completion update. Cleanup is mandatory
on success and failure.

Evidence and scope
==================

A direct adversarial test mutates the capacity report after capture and proves
the origin recheck rejects it. Readiness requires
`production_checkpoint_capacity_materializer_run_pair_snapshotted_and_origins_rechecked=true`.
No Cargo, proof, Anvil, network, signing, deployment, funds operation, commit or
push was run.
