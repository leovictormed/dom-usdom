USD0M stage 515 — checkpoint outer-prover input and receipt snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The long-running checkpoint outer prover consumed the live planner surface,
outer job, statement, chain, segment receipts and guest artifacts after Cargo
compilation. It also resumed and published directly in the durable output
directory. Concurrent replacement could mix filesystem generations or leave a
completion index beside a partial output.

Correction
==========

The launcher now captures the complete stage-514 planner surface, outer job
index, statement and chain, both segment and outer ELF/program/ImageID files,
and any resumable outer receipt into one owner-only layout replica. It rejects
empty, excessive, duplicate or noncanonical derived catalogs before copying
their files. The prover runs only in the replica. After successful built-in
verification, the receipt is installed durably and the verified receipt-set
index is installed last as the sole completion marker. A prior marker is
removed and its directory synchronized before work starts, so interruption
fails closed.

Evidence and scope
==================

Focused operator-runbook tests enforce capture order, bounded unique catalogs,
replica-only proving, resumable receipt capture and completion-marker order.
Readiness requires
`production_checkpoint_outer_prover_inputs_and_receipts_snapshotted=true`.
No proof, network, signing, deployment, funds operation, commit or push was
performed.
