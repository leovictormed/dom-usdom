SAFE DEPLOYMENT FINAL RECEIPT BARRIER — STAGE 1174
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The three-Safe executor validated receipt consensus and installed a missing
receipt, but did not reread that new durable pathname. It also did not recheck
all three installed receipts after the final source/package rebuild. A replaced
or partially materialized receipt could therefore escape the final success
barrier even though the on-chain Safe target itself was correct.

Correction
==========

Every per-role receipt is now reread through the private-file boundary directly
after create-once installation and compared byte-for-byte with the quorum
projection. At final completion, all three receipt and validation files are
reread, each receipt is independently reprojected by the strict checker, and
the canonical output must equal its installed bytes before the corresponding
Safe target can be accepted.
