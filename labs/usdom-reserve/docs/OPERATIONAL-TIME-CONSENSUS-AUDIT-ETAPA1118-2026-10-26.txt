OPERATIONAL TIME CONSENSUS AUDIT — ETAPA 1118
Date: 2026-10-26
Scope: lightweight local Node validation. No network, Cargo/Forge, signing, deployment, funds, commit or push.

RESULT
The finalized multisource preflight now rejects grossly wrong or unstable host time before it can authorize downstream fee, certificate-deadline or signing decisions.

CONTROLS
- Every bounded RPC request compares wall-clock progress with the monotonic clock.
  Wall-clock rollback, monotonic rollback or more than five seconds of divergence
  aborts the provider.
- Finalized block responses must contain canonical number, hash and timestamp.
  The timestamp is sampled again from the exact pinned block after collection and
  must remain identical.
- Two to five source samples require distinct source IDs, monotonic local
  collection intervals, no more than 15 seconds finalized timestamp skew, and no
  more than 30 seconds local collection skew.
- A finalized timestamp may be at most 30 minutes old and no more than 15 seconds
  ahead of the local clock. These limits make the preflight unavailable during a
  prolonged finality outage instead of allowing time-sensitive actions.
- The authority recheck already runs immediately before each signing or broadcast
  authorization, so rollback/divergence blocks those downstream decisions.

LIMIT
Ethereum block timestamps are consensus fields with bounded proposer discretion;
they are not trusted civil time. Comparing independent RPC reports detects drift,
staleness and operational misconfiguration, but does not create trusted time or
prove a correct host clock. Certificate validity remains enforced by Node TLS
against the host clock.
