Date: 2026-10-23
Scope: crates/dom-rpc/src/lib.rs

Finding corrected
=================
`GET /peers` required the bearer token but was added outside the
`auth_read_routes` governor. It was consequently the only authenticated read
capability that a caller could invoke without consuming the configured read
bucket. The response is bounded indirectly by the node's peer policy and the
listener is loopback-only, but the exception still bypassed the explicit RPC
resource policy and allowed a credential-holding local client or proxy to
repeatedly clone and serialize the peer inventory.

Correction
==========
`/peers` now belongs to `auth_read_routes` and shares that router's read
GovernorLayer. It therefore uses the same validated burst policy and
kernel-observed peer identity as mempool, transaction, wallet balance, chain
scan, build-information and shutdown reads. Authentication remains outside the
protected route group, so an unauthenticated request cannot reach the handler.

Coverage audit
==============
Every public confirmed-chain read except the deliberately constant-time
`/health` liveness probe belongs to the public read governor. Every
credentialed read now belongs to the authenticated read governor or a
capability-specific read governor. Every mutation has its own submit governor;
proof-verifying and mining mutations additionally retain their body, timeout
and concurrency controls.
