Stage 468 — authenticated-checkpoint durable RPC-source binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The authenticated-checkpoint observer validated runtime endpoints against the
public preflight catalog and required identical finalized observations, but its
durable report retained only the first observation. Operator identities and
origin commitments were discarded after consensus.

Remediation
===========

The preflight RPC module now derives a domain-separated commitment over the
ordered catalog entries. The durable checkpoint observation report includes
the exact canonical operator/origin list, source count and source-set
commitment. Its catalog is among the exact inputs reread before create-once
publication, so a catalog change during observation still fails closed.

Evidence boundary
=================

`production_authenticated_checkpoint_durable_report_binds_rpc_source_set` is a
structural fact. It does not claim a live checkpoint observation. No network,
signing, deployment, funds, mining, commit or push occurred.
