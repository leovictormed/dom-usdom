USDⓄM RESERVE — BROADCAST RECEIPT PRE-JOURNAL (STAGE 416)
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding

Safe and contract deployment receipts were written to predictable `/tmp`
paths. Ordinary redirection could replace prior evidence. A write failure could
also be discovered only after a hardware-wallet broadcast had already begun,
creating dangerous retry ambiguity.

Correction

- Every receipt now lives in the owner-only control-plane evidence directory.
- Shell noclobber mode and a 0600 umask reserve a new receipt inode and keep its
  descriptor open before `cast send` starts.
- Broadcast output is written through that already-open descriptor and synced
  before it is parsed through the strict private-file reader.
- A failed command retains its attempt journal and explicitly requires nonce
  reconciliation before any retry.
- Rebuild output may overwrite only fresh `mktemp` files via explicit `>|`;
  authority and receipt paths remain create-only.
- Regression forbids the former receipt paths and requires all three documented
  broadcast loops to pre-open, sync and privately read their journals.

Validation

- all 37 runbook Bash blocks parse;
- operator/runbook suite: 64/64 passed;
- complete Node regression before the subsequent legacy-SDK removal: 306/306 passed;
- the active non-development finalized-burn proof was not changed or restarted.

No push, deployment, signature, transaction broadcast, funds, or mining was
performed.
