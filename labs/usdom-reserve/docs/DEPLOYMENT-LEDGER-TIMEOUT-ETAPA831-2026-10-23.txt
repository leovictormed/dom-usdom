ETAPA 831 — mainnet deployment Ledger identity timeout
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The mainnet deployment executor already authenticated Cast and GNU timeout and
bounded its RPC/derivation children, but the Ledger deployer identity check
called Cast directly. A stalled device interaction could hold the deployment
lock, private package and RPC proxy indefinitely immediately before gas planning
and publication.

Correction

- The Ledger identity read now uses the already authenticated exact timeout
  binary with foreground semantics, a five-minute deadline, TERM and KILL after
  five seconds.
- Cast and timeout identities are checked immediately before and after the call.
- Timeout/signal failure propagates through the existing status-preserving
  cleanup, which terminates/waits for the RPC proxy and erases private state.

Validation

- Bash syntax passes.
- Deployment execution, tool parity/pinning, strict child framing, environment,
  descriptor, timeout and cleanup suites: 33/33 pass.
- Diff check passes.
- No Ledger, RPC, signing, broadcast, deployment, commit, push or funds action ran.
