Stage 661 — ERC-20 bounded-call and atomicity audit (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited proof-reserve interactions with USDT-compatible ERC-20 contracts during
`balanceOf`, `transferFrom` and `transfer`: revert, false/malformed/oversized
returndata, gas grief, hidden balance inconsistencies, callbacks/reentrancy,
recipient restrictions, donations and rollback of debt/bucket/accounting state.

Confirmed defect

Balance reads and SafeERC20 transfers used high-level wrappers that could copy
attacker-selected returndata. Existing exact-balance postconditions prevented
false credit and accounting loss, and EVM rollback preserved state on failure,
but a hostile token could return very large data and force unnecessary memory
expansion or gas consumption at the reserve boundary.

Correction

- `USDOMProofReserve` now gives token balance and transfer calls a fixed
  500,000-gas budget and copies at most one 32-byte word.
- `balanceOf` must succeed with exactly one ABI word.
- Transfers accept either zero returndata, for historical USDT compatibility,
  or exactly canonical boolean `true`. Revert, false, malformed, short,
  trailing and oversized replies become `UnsupportedToken`.
- The external transfer still occurs inside `nonReentrant`, and the existing
  exact before/after balance equations remain authoritative. A lying, fee,
  rebasing-like or callback token cannot create credited deposits or consume a
  payout without exact movement.
- All effects written before payout transfer remain transaction-atomic: a token
  failure restores paid flags, request/burn bindings, pending links/count/amount,
  released total and rate bucket.
- Recipient self/reserve/token exclusions and donation accounting remain
  unchanged. Donations never increment `totalDeposited` and cannot authorize
  native issuance or payment beyond credited outstanding value.

Material regressions

- A one-megabyte transfer/transferFrom return bomb rolls back a deposit and a
  queued payout. The failed deposit ID remains unused; debt, bucket and pending
  amount remain available; normal token behavior permits exact retry.
- A one-megabyte `balanceOf` return bomb likewise cannot create credit or consume
  debt, and the original payout succeeds after the read boundary is restored.

Validation

- new hostile-token regressions: 2/2 passed;
- complete Solidity campaign: 165/165 passed across 14 suites, including all
  invariant campaigns;
- post-format hostile-token regressions: 2/2 passed;
- production lint, formatting and `git diff --check`: passed.

No Rust, Cargo, Node or RISC Zero code was touched or executed. No commit,
push, deployment, signature, transmission or funds operation was performed.
