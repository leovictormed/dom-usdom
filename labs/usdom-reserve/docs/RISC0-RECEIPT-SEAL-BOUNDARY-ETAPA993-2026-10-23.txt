RISC ZERO RECEIPT / SEAL BOUNDARY — ETAPA993
Date: 2026-10-23

Result
------
The host and Solidity cryptographic formats agree: both guests publish exactly
the statement bytes as the journal; hosts verify receipt journal and ImageID,
require the pinned Groth16 verifier-parameter digest, then package the raw eight
BN254 proof words behind selector 0x73c457ba. Both adapters compute SHA-256 over
the exact 32 statement bytes, require 260 bytes and the same selector, bind the
guest ImageID immutably, call the direct verifier with 4,500,000 gas, and accept
only success with empty returndata. The direct verifier is responsible for
canonical BN254 field/curve validation.

Concrete evidence gap fixed
---------------------------
The authenticated artifact collectors validated selector, seal size, ImageID,
journal digest and Groth16 parameters, while each isolated release E2E passed
the collected seal directly to the adapter and bounded only the overall
transaction at 5,000,000 gas. Consequently the release rehearsal did not state
or independently prove its assumptions about the adapter's inner 4,500,000 gas
cap and on-chain journal-digest computation.

Both finalized-burn and checkpoint E2Es now independently reject a seal unless
it is exactly 260 bytes with selector 0x73c457ba. After adapter deployment they
read `RISC_ZERO_VERIFIER_GAS_LIMIT()` and require 4,500,000, call
`journalDigest(statementHash)`, independently compute SHA-256 over the raw
32-byte statement, and require equality before proof verification. A successful
call against the observed direct runtime also proves the void-return ABI because
the adapter rejects any nonempty returndata.
