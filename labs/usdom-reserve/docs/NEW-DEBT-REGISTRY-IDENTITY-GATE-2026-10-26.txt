NEW DEBT REGISTRY IDENTITY GATE — 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

USDOMProofReserve revalidated the immutable checkpoint-registry dependency for
deposits and terminal shutdown, but the first acceptance of a finalized-burn
proof did not repeat that check. That first acceptance creates an irreversible
collateral liability. If the selected registry runtime or its committed
configuration identity diverged, the reserve could still ask the burn verifier
to authorize a new debt even though it no longer recognized the checkpoint
authority selected at deployment.

This is distinct from registry halt or graceful closure. A halt freezes future
checkpoint progression but does not invalidate finalized burns already proved
against the last accepted state. Those debts must remain claimable.

Fix
===

The non-renewal branch of queueWithdrawal now calls
_requireCheckpointRegistryUnchanged() after structural/finality checks and
before any token probe, proof verification or permanent debt mutation.

The check intentionally does not run for renewal or executeWithdrawal. Once a
valid proof has created a debt, registry failure, emergency halt or later
identity drift cannot be used to strand the beneficiary. Verifier and token
identity checks remain independently enforced in those paths.

Validation
==========

The new regression mutates checkpointConfigHash and proves that:

1. a fresh burn cannot create totalProvedDebtAmount and reverts RegistryChanged;
2. after restoring the identity and proving another burn, subsequent registry
   drift does not prevent its delayed payout.

The focused regression passes. The complete offline Forge suite exits 0.
USDOMProofReserve runtime is 20,633 bytes, leaving 3,943 bytes under EIP-170;
DOMCheckpointRegistry remains 10,418 bytes. The whitespace check passes.

No Rust/Cargo, operational script, network, signing, deployment, funds, commit
or push was used.
