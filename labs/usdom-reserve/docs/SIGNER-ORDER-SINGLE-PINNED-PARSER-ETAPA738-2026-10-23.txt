ETAPA 738 — signer ordering inside the pinned parser
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
The release/native signing ceremony passed owner addresses from pinned JQ into `sort` resolved
through PATH. Sort order determines Safe signature concatenation, so this remained an authority
transform outside the pinned parser boundary.

Correction
==========
Lowercasing and canonical ordering now occur in one expression inside the already pinned JQ.
The external `sort` dependency and pipeline were removed. Existing strict increasing-order checks
remain and fail closed on duplicate or malformed owners.

Validation
==========
Combined signing structural tests pass 3/3 and prohibit the external ordering pipeline. Shell
syntax and diff checks pass. No ceremony, Ledger, RPC or signing ran.

No commit, push, deploy, transmission or funds were used.
