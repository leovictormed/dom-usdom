USD-native / USDOM — Stage 427: canonical proof size at value entrypoints
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The immutable RISC Zero adapters rejected every EVM seal whose length was not
260 bytes, but USDOMProofReserve.queueWithdrawal and
DOMCheckpointRegistry.submitCheckpointCandidate admitted any nonempty proof up
to 64 KiB before invoking those adapters. Such input could not gain authority,
but it unnecessarily crossed the main contract ABI/parser and consumed control
checks and calldata processing before deterministic rejection.

Correction
==========

Both main contracts now expose EVM_PROOF_BYTES=260 and reject every other
length before verifier interaction. MAX_PROOF_BYTES remains as a compatibility
alias to the same exact value. This produces one consistent executable policy
from the main entrypoint through the immutable adapter, Rust packager, artifact
collector and signed verifier manifest.

Solidity fixtures, fuzz handlers and stateful invariants now use the exact
selector-prefixed eight-word shape. Explicit 259-byte and 261-byte cases prove
early rejection by the reserve and registry themselves.
