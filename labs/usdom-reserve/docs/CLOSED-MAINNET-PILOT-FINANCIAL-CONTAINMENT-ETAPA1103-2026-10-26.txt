USD0M closed-mainnet pilot financial containment — Stage 1103 — 2026-10-26

The pilot already fixed the asset amount at 1,000,000 USDT base units for the
deposit and redemption, bounded EIP-1559 fee/gas per transaction, required two
mainnet RPCs and rechecked receipt finality. It lacked one independent
cumulative ETH budget. A sequence of individually valid transactions could
therefore exceed the intended aggregate pilot exposure.

Implemented caps

- deposit transaction: <= 0.05 ETH; at most 3; phase <= 0.15 ETH
- redemption transaction: <= 0.10 ETH; at most 2; phase <= 0.20 ETH
- entire pilot signed worst case: <= 0.35 ETH

Both executors aggregate all prior signed validation records and the proposed
transaction before the signature prompt. They recheck all durable validations
again before broadcast. The offline phase checker independently derives the
same budget from hash/plan/signer/block-bound receipt chains and publishes the
summary in its checklist. Noncanonical, zero, extra-step and over-cap costs are
rejected.

The preceding Stage 1102 bindings remain mandatory: exact release SHA/config,
USDT/reserve, funder/recipient, burn/request, chain 1, calldata, signer, target,
successful receipt and two-RPC finalized identity. No missing evidence was
created or accepted.

Validation: pinned Node suites 14/14 passed across budget, funding, receipt,
state and financial evidence; both executor scripts pass bash syntax; diff
checks pass. No network, Cargo, Forge, key access, signing, deployment, commit
or push ran.
