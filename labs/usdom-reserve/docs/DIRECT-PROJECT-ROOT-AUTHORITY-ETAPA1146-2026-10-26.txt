DIRECT PROJECT ROOT AUTHORITY — STAGE 1146
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding

`readDirectProjectFile` required the lexical project root to equal its realpath,
and `readDirectFile` authenticated the target plus its immediate parent. It did
not hold and authenticate the project-root directory itself. A group/other-
writable root was therefore outside the explicit authority checks, and root
replacement during collection was not independently detected at this layer.

Correction

The project reader now opens the exact root with O_DIRECTORY|O_NOFOLLOW and
requires it to be:

- a directory owned by the current effective user;
- not writable by group or others;
- the exact lexical root when resolved through its open descriptor.

It retains that descriptor through the target read, then compares device,
inode, owner and mode before/after and against a newly opened named root. A
rename/replacement, permission/owner change, symlink or non-directory boundary
fails closed. The existing target and immediate-parent inode, link, permission,
size, realpath and content-stability checks remain in force.

Validation

- Active repository and component roots: owner `leonardov`, mode 0700.
- Added negative test for a 0770 project root and positive recovery after 0700.
- Node syntax and focused direct-file/evidence/readiness tests: PASS, 15/15.

No production fact changed. No Cargo, Rust, Forge, network, signing, deployment,
funds, commit or push ran.
