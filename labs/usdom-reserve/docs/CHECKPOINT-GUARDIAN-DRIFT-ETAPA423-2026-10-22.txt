USDOM stage 423 — checkpoint guardian drift fails closed
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding

DOMCheckpointRegistry authenticated the immutable guardian identity and Safe
policy during construction and before permanent halt. Checkpoint submission and
finalization, however, only rechecked the verifier. A later change to the
guardian Safe implementation, threshold, owners, module, guard or required
mainnet fallback handler could therefore leave checkpoint progression enabled
while making the emergency halt path unavailable.

Correction

The registry now applies one shared _requireGuardianUnchanged predicate before
both submitCheckpointCandidate and finalizeCheckpoint, and reuses the same
predicate for haltPermanently. Any drift in the immutable guardian runtime,
Safe singleton, singleton code, 2-of-3 owner policy, empty module/guard policy
or mainnet fallback handler stops checkpoint progression before proof work or
state mutation.

Evidence

- New regression proves threshold drift rejects submission and module drift
  rejects finalization while preserving the pending candidate and predecessor.
- Complete Solidity suite: 146/146 passed, including all reserve and checkpoint
  invariants.
- Production Solidity lint passed.

- Complete Node regression: 307/307 passed.
- Complete provenance passed, including 169 imported files with 16 declared
  changes and the ordered SHA-256 manifest of 1,207 source files.

No RPC, signature, transaction, deployment, funds, mining, commit, tag or push
