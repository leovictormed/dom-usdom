USDOM hardening stage 388 — operator captured public reads

Date: 2026-10-22

The seven closed-mainnet operator commands for finalized-deposit registration
and observation, checkpoint recovery, private-burn preparation and submission,
TPM keystore operation and TPM authority bootstrap no longer carry local
pathname-inspect/open/read implementations.

Owner-only files continue through the shared zeroizing private reader. Public
witnesses, indexes and node identity artifacts now go through the shared
descriptor-captured reader, which retains and authenticates the exact parent,
opens the child with openat/no-follow, enforces owner/single-link/bounds, and
reauthenticates all identities after reading. The explicit allow-empty variant
is available only for formats that intentionally permit an empty payload; the
TPM owner authorization remains the only operational use.

Validation completed:

- shared captured-reader test passes 1/1, including distinct empty rejection
  and explicit allow-empty acceptance;
- all seven affected operator binaries compile together, offline and with one
  build job, under native-usdom-v4-poc plus usdom-wallet-operator;
- the final combined check is warning-free.

The detached capacity proof was not restarted and retains its pre-stage binary.
