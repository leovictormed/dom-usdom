ETAPA 749 — append-only Safe signature assembly
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
Safe-recovery signing used PATH-resolved `mv` to replace the accumulating signature JSON after
each Ledger result. This subprocess controlled the exact signatures delivered to final transaction
assembly and could substitute or redirect the authority artifact.

Correction
==========
Every accumulator version now has a unique private filename derived from signer index and role.
Pinned JQ writes the next version create-once in practice, mode is set owner-only, and the shell
advances its reference without renaming or replacing prior versions. External `mv` is absent.

Validation
==========
Signing and durable-writer suites pass 10/10. Structural tests require versioned accumulator paths
and reject PATH-resolved `mv`. Shell syntax and diff checks pass. No ceremony or signing ran.

No Ledger, RPC, commit, push, deploy, transmission or funds were used.
