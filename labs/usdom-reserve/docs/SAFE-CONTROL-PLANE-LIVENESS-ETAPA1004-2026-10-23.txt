ETAPA 1004 — Safe control-plane authority and liveness — 2026-10-23

Scope
- Production reserve governance/guardian and checkpoint-registry guardian.
- Constructor identity, Safe singleton/runtime, threshold, owners, modules,
  guard, fallback handler, pause/resume/halt ordering and stale emergencies.

Concrete finding and correction
- The contracts committed to getOwners() linked-list order. Safe authority is
  the 2-of-3 owner set, not its enumeration order. Removing and re-adding the
  same owners can reorder the list and previously made control-plane health
  fail forever, stranding deposits, payouts, checkpoints and resume despite no
  authority change.
- Both contracts now validate the three returned owners exactly as before, then
  sort their addresses before hashing. Actual owner replacement still changes
  the commitment; permutation of the same set preserves liveness.
- The shared SDK safeOwnersCommitment now performs the identical numeric address
  sort. Its regression expects all permutations to have one commitment.
- Solidity regressions exercise reordered governance and guardian owners while
  retaining healthy value/checkpoint control paths.

Other audit conclusions
- Mainnet constructors require Safe proxy identity, pinned proxy runtime,
  singleton and singleton runtime, exact 2-of-3 distinct EOA owners, no modules
  or guard, and the reviewed fallback handler/runtime.
- Operational value and checkpoint paths repeat the complete identity. Drift
  fails closed while pending payout/candidate state remains retryable after an
  exact authority restoration.
- Pause may remain callable after identity drift as an emergency fail-safe;
  resume requires healthy governance and guardian, a fresh delayed schedule,
  verifier identity and token policy. A later pause clears the schedule.
- Queue-generation-bound pause rejects stale automation. The registry halt is
  irreversible and requires the exact healthy guardian; guardian drift already
  stops candidate submission/finalization, so loss of the explicit halt call
  cannot leave checkpoint progression open.
