USD/DOM stage 432 — Safe-evidence byte binding through release authorization
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The release-signing validator fully checked the semantic Safe recovery bundle,
its governance address and ordered owner set. It did not compare the exact
bundle bytes with the SHA-256 document identity stored in the release evidence.
An internally valid historical or substituted bundle retaining those public
identities could therefore reach the Ledger boundary. The final EIP-1271
materializer also rebuilt the authorization without repeating the full bundle
validation or retaining that bundle in its shell-level input snapshot.

Correction
==========

The fixed offline helper now hashes the direct 0600 bundle snapshot and passes
that identity to the validator. The validator requires the exact release
evidence document path, exact SHA-256, matching Ethereum chain, complete Safe
recovery semantics, current release governance and exact ordered owners. It
also rejects malformed or noncanonical finalized-anchor block fields.

The final authorization materializer reads the same direct Safe artifact,
repeats the complete request validation before querying finalized EIP-1271
state, rereads it as part of its fixed input set and keeps a separate byte
snapshot through every output publication. Deletion and replacement during
materialization therefore fail closed.
