Stage 466 — deployment-identity RPC-origin binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The deployment-identity materializer verified runtime RPC URLs against the
hash-addressed catalog, but its durable observation retained only operator IDs.
The exact catalog origin commitments used for the nonce and empty-address
observations were discarded, so later review could not bind each result to its
endpoint origin.

Remediation
===========

Deployment identity observation now requires one distinct, nonzero canonical
origin commitment for every provider and stores it beside that provider's
operator ID and finalized observation. The materializer supplies the ordered
`originKeccak256` values directly from the catalog whose exact bytes it already
rechecks after observation. Missing, malformed, duplicate or count-mismatched
origin commitments fail closed.

Evidence boundary
=================

`production_mainnet_deployment_identity_observation_binds_rpc_origins` is a
structural fact. It does not claim a live deployment observation. No network,
signing, deployment, funds, mining, commit or push occurred.
