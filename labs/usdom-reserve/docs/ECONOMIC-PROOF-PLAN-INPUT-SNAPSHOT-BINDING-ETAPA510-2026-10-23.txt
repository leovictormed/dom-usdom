USD0M stage 510 — economic proof-plan input snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The economic planner checked two live indexes before `cargo run`, then reopened
them, every private economic source component and six chunk/block guest
artifacts after compilation. A replacement could produce a plan from a source
view different from the one present when the operator started the command.

Correction
==========

Both indexes are captured first. A bounded canonical catalog is derived from
their fixed bytes and all segment private inputs, block transitions, chunk
transitions and chunk witnesses are captured into an owner-only layout replica.
The chunk and block ELF/program/ImageID triplets are copied into the same
replica and remain checked against the host's compiled SHA pins. Planning runs
only there, and its single job index is captured and durably installed.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook suite asserts the private reader, isolated working root,
both guest families and durable publication. Readiness requires
`production_economic_proof_plan_inputs_snapshotted=true`. No proof generation,
network access, signing, deployment or funds operation occurs in this stage.
