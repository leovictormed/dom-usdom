ETAPA 873 — WALLET OPERATOR CHILD PROCESS LIFECYCLE
Date: 2026-10-23

Scope and finding
-----------------
The audit was restricted to finalized-deposit wallet observation/registration,
closed-mainnet private-burn submission and finalized-deposit witness building.
Pinning logic was preserved. Each flow had status-preserving secret cleanup and
no background jobs, but Cargo could retain compiler descendants indefinitely,
and the credential-bearing wallet/runtime binaries had no outer deadline.

Correction
----------
- Cargo build/run commands now execute in timeout-created process groups with a
  one-hour deadline and 30-second TERM-to-KILL escalation.
- TPM keystore unlock and the observer/register/submit binaries now have
  five-minute deadlines and five-second kill escalation.
- Existing Curl ten-second network bounds, tool identity rechecks, temporary
  secret cleanup and signal exit statuses remain unchanged.

Validation
----------
- Bash syntax passed for all four scoped scripts.
- Lifecycle, Cargo pinning, Curl boundary and secret-cleanup regressions passed
  after reconciliation; the final affected suites contain 17 passing tests and
  no remaining failure.
- Diff whitespace validation passed.
- No Cargo build, RPC, TPM, signing, submission or fund operation ran.
