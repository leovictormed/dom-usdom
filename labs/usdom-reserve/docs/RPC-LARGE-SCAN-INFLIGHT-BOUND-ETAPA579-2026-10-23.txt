RPC large-scan in-flight bound — stage 579
Date: 2026-10-23
Scope: crates/dom-rpc/src/{lib.rs,middleware.rs}

Finding corrected
=================
The authenticated chain scan, full-fidelity scan, private transfer/burn lookup
and native USDOM history endpoints bounded each page and were rate limited, but
had no shared in-flight response-generation bound. The default read burst is
100 and the newly bounded operator maximum is 10,000. A credential-holding
client could therefore admit many concurrent page requests, each performing a
chain snapshot and potentially constructing a 2 MiB or 8 MiB-class response.
Per-page bounds prevent one request from growing without limit; they did not
bound aggregate simultaneous generation.

Correction
==========
All five potentially large authenticated scan routes now share exactly two
response-generation slots. A middleware uses `Semaphore::try_acquire_owned`:
when both slots are occupied, another page is immediately answered with HTTP
503 rather than being queued. The owned permit covers the complete handler and
JSON response construction and is released on success, error, timeout or task
cancellation.

The shared read governor remains outside this capacity gate, so over-quota
traffic receives 429 before it can acquire a response slot. Bearer
authentication remains outermost. Public confirmed-chain point lookups,
`/health`, status and small authenticated administrative reads are not routed
through the new scanner capacity and retain their existing behavior. Page
clients already treat overloaded scan operations as retryable.

Boundary retained
=================
The permit covers snapshot work and response serialization. It does not remain
held for an arbitrarily slow client after the materialized response is returned
to Hyper; transport backpressure and the per-source governor govern that phase.
The per-page block-count, consensus block-size and encoded soft budgets remain
the primary response-size bounds.
