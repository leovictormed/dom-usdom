ETAPA 1086 — OUTFLOW BUCKET / TIMESTAMP ARITHMETIC AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-26

Scope
-----
Adversarial review of USDOMProofReserve's global outflow bucket, fractional
refill, withdrawal delay, lease expiry, pause/resume time, multiple permanent
debts, execution order and every uint64/uint256 timestamp conversion. No deploy,
broadcast, signing, funds, network access, commit or push was performed.

Findings
--------
No rate-limit bypass, arithmetic truncation or debt-ordering loss was found.

* Bucket balance and capacity are uint64, and every narrowing assignment is
  preceded by an explicit bound. `availableOutflow` returns early once elapsed
  time reaches one day, so its only multiplication has elapsed < 86,400 and a
  uint64 capacity and cannot overflow uint256.
* Successful execution checkpoints the computed balance and current timestamp.
  Failed verifier, token-policy, solvency, transfer or post-transfer checks
  revert the complete bucket update.
* Fractional refill rounds down and resetting its timestamp after a withdrawal
  can only discard a sub-unit remainder; it cannot mint capacity.
* Queue and administrative deadlines use `_timestampAfter` and fail closed
  before uint64 overflow. Exact readyAt and expiresAt boundaries are inclusive
  in the intended direction; pruning starts only after expiry.
* Pause invalidates queue leases but does not edit bucket state. Ethereum time
  continues to refill the bucket at the same one-day rate, capped at capacity;
  pause/resume therefore cannot manufacture more than the normal burst cap.
* Debts share one global bucket. Permissionless execution can choose which
  ready debt consumes available capacity, but changing order cannot increase
  aggregate payout. Permanent debt accounting prevents expiry or pause from
  freeing collateral.
* An old `validUntil` is intentionally admissible after native consensus has
  made the burn irreversible. Native admission already bounds that deadline to
  its authenticated Ethereum anchor; the EVM queue uses a separate seven-day
  renewable lease. Treating the original deadline as permanent debt erasure
  would strand collateral rather than improve solvency.

Regressions prepared
--------------------
Three focused tests were added:

1. two half-capacity debts in adversarial execution order after a full-capacity
   payout, proving exact fractional refill and that a failed attempt consumes no
   bucket;
2. pause immediately after bucket exhaustion, proving pause neither resets nor
   freezes the normal refill clock; and
3. resume scheduling at uint64 max, proving custom fail-closed overflow and no
   partial administrative state.
