Stage 492 — Deployment executor package-snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

The deployment executor accepted USDOM_PACKAGE_SHA256 independently from the
package path. Although the handoff recomputed and checked the digest, the
caller still controlled two representations of one semantic input and the
executor repeatedly reopened the caller's original pathname across a ceremony
with human delays.

The executor now directly snapshots the owner-only package into its private
temporary directory at ceremony start, derives the SHA-256 from those exact
bytes, and directs every handoff and raw transaction validation to the same
snapshot. The operator runbook no longer exports a separately calculated
digest. Omission, substitution and pathname TOCTOU fail closed at the snapshot
boundary.

`production_deployment_executor_package_sha_derived_from_snapshot` is a
structural fact. No network access, hardware signature, transaction, funds,
mining, commit or push occurred while producing this evidence.

Validation: complete operator runbook 71/71; readiness 60/60.
