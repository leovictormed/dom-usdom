Stage 615 — proof-reserve queue state-machine audit (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited `USDOMProofReserve` after the permanent debt binding introduced in
stage 612, focusing on `totalPendingAmount`, `pendingCount`, the intrusive
pending linked list, expiry cleanup, arbitrary-order discard, emergency queue
epoch invalidation, exact debt renewal and identifier reuse.

Result

No additional contract defect was confirmed. The state transitions preserve
the intended invariants:

- queue admission appends one current-epoch record and increments both the
  count and reserved amount exactly once;
- execution, expiry discard and pruning unlink a current-epoch record and
  decrement both values exactly once;
- emergency pause advances `queueEpoch` and clears current aggregate/list
  state in O(1), while cleanup of old-epoch records does not subtract the new
  generation's aggregates;
- an exact renewal may overwrite the same `burnId` storage slot from an old
  epoch, but arbitrary-order old-epoch cleanup observes the renewed record as
  current and cannot delete or unlink it;
- `pendingRequests` is deleted only when it still points to the record being
  removed, while the stage-612 permanent burn/statement and request/burn
  bindings survive every temporary cleanup; and
- duplicate and unknown prune candidates are idempotently skipped.

Regression added

`testOldEpochCleanupCannotEraseExactRenewalOrCorruptMixedQueue` exercises the
storage-aliasing boundary directly:

1. queue three finalized debts, pause, and invalidate their queue generation;
2. resume and renew the middle debt under the exact same burn/request IDs;
3. append another current debt, then batch-prune old, current, duplicate and
   unknown candidates in mixed order;
4. verify both links of the two-entry current queue, head/tail, request indexes,
   permanent bindings, count and amount;
5. renew a previously pruned debt, remove the middle current record, prune the
   remaining expired head sequence, and verify the empty state; and
6. renew the final old debt after both epoch invalidation and expiry cleanup.
