USDOM stage 517 — finalized-burn prover snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The long-running finalized-burn prover reopened its planned input and index,
recursive checkpoint assumption and guest artifacts after compilation and
between composite proving and succinct compression. Concurrent replacement
could mix filesystem generations or make a resumable receipt authorize a
different job.

Correction
==========

The command captures the stage-516 source, job input and canonical job index,
checkpoint statement, verified checkpoint-outer receipt, finalized-burn guest
ELF/program and ImageID, and any existing succinct or composite resumable
receipt into one owner-only replica. It enforces fixed canonical job paths,
source/input equality, catalog hashes and explicit byte bounds before proving.
The prover runs only from the replica. Any prior completion index is invalidated
and synchronized before the long operation; after success the verified succinct
receipt is durably installed first and its receipt index is installed last.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook regression checks complete capture, catalog validation,
replica-only proving, resumable receipt capture and output-last publication.
Readiness requires
production_finalized_burn_prover_inputs_and_receipts_snapshotted=true. No proof,
network, signature, deployment or funds operation is performed.
