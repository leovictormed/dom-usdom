ETAPA1036 — closed mainnet redemption executor

Implemented the missing operational bridge from authenticated native burn proof to
the reserve payout. The canonical package accepts only Ethereum chain 1 and exactly
1,000,000 base units, derives immutable queue/execute calldata, and keeps
broadcastAuthorized=false.

The interactive executor uses Ledger only, zero-value EIP-1559 transactions, exact
nonces and calldata, bounded gas/fees/maximum cost, separate literal authorization
for signing and publishing each operation, and two independent public RPCs. Queue
receipt finality precedes readyAt observation. Early execution exits retryably;
stored raw transactions and receipts are revalidated on rerun. Both receipts require
matching finalized block/hash observations and terminal paidBurns/paidRequests state.
The evidence record is written last.
