USD + DOM — reserve governance and economic-policy bounds audit
Stage 554 — 2026-10-23

Scope
-----
Reviewed USDOMProofReserve and the prototype USDOMReserve constructor policy,
pause/resume authority, withdrawal and governance delays, exposure ceiling,
24-hour token-bucket capacity, immutable deployment commitments, the fixed
one-USDT pilot materializer, and the JavaScript configuration gates that feed
deployment tooling.

Finding and correction
----------------------
Both Solidity constructors declare withdrawalDelay, governanceDelay,
maxOutstanding and outflowCapacity as uint64. The final deployment-commitment
calculator already parsed all four as uint64, and the Solidity ABI therefore
could not deploy an out-of-range value. However, validateConfiguration and
validateProofConfiguration initially parsed them as unrestricted uint256.

Consequently, an exposure or capacity at or above 2^64 could pass the first
syntax/policy gate and fail only at the later commitment or ABI-encoding stage.
This was a fail-closed deployment inconsistency rather than a route to an
incorrect mainnet contract, but it made validation non-compositional and could
waste an operator ceremony after earlier acceptance.

Both preliminary validators now parse all four constructor parameters directly
as uint64. Focused regression vectors assert rejection of 2^64 independently
for every field in both the prototype and proof-authorized configurations.

Invariant assessment
--------------------
- Solidity enforces withdrawal delay in [6 hours, 3 days] and governance delay
  in [2 days, 30 days]. The maximum withdrawal delay plus the one-hour minimum
  execution window remains below the seven-day queue lease.
- Exposure and outflow are nonzero uint64 values, and outflow cannot exceed
  maximum outstanding credit.
- The rate limiter starts at one burst capacity, refills linearly over 24 hours,
  caps at capacity and is not reset by pause/resume. The proof reserve also
  invalidates the pending queue generation on the first emergency pause.
- Governance and guardian cannot change economic parameters after deployment.
  Resume requires governance scheduling, the immutable governance delay, and
  fresh control-plane, verifier and token-policy checks.
- Mainnet constructor policy is included in the preactivation/deployment config
  hash and is pinned into production bytecode. A policy change therefore needs
  a separately reviewed deployment and native activation rather than an admin
  transaction.
- The closed pilot materializer fixes both maximum outstanding credit and daily
  burst capacity to 1,000,000 base units (1 USDT), preserves both authorization
  flags as false and rejects a pre-existing conflicting policy.

Validation
----------
`node --test test/node/configuration.test.mjs` passed 7/7, including the new
eight uint64 overflow cases. Inspection found no parameter mutation, bucket
reset, delay bypass, governance sweep or policy-hash omission. No Cargo,
Foundry, network, proof-runner, deployment, signing or funds operation ran.

Residual scope
--------------
The chosen one-USDT pilot values are a release policy decision; this audit
proves their exact encoding and enforcement, not an economic claim that any
larger future limit is safe. Full integrated regression remains pending until
the active RandomX proof releases local resources.
