ETAPA735 — partial recovery finalized-anchor authentication
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding and correction
======================

Completion manifests recorded the all-RPC finalized block established by
ETAPA732, but recovery validated only its shape. It proved current receipt
finality without asking whether each RPC still recognized the stored finalized
anchor. A forged manifest or a violation of Ethereum finalized ancestry could
therefore avoid direct detection at that boundary.

For every predecessor and every independent RPC, recovery now reads the stored
finalized number/hash from the strict private manifest, requires the current
finalized head not to regress below it, fetches the block by the exact stored
number, and compares both returned number and hash. Absence, reorg, divergence
or finality regression fails before nonce, runtime or next-role authorization.
The manifest remains bound to the current package/release/observation/catalog
and is byte-rechecked after the RPC sequence.
