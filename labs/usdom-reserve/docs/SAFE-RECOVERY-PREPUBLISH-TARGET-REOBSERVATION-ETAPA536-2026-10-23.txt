Stage 536 — Safe recovery prepublication target reobservation
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The recovery executor authenticated the EIP-712 Safe transaction, recovered and
canonically ordered the exact two surviving owners, bound chain 1, Safe address
and nonce zero, and checked the target Safe before signing. Before publication
it reread all local evidence and the authenticated RPC catalog, but it did not
repeat the target-Safe consensus observation. Ledger signing and operator review
are unbounded intervals. A concurrent Safe execution could therefore change the
Safe nonce or owner policy while leaving every local signed artifact unchanged.

Correction

For each governance, reserveGuardian and checkpointGuardian recovery, the
executor now reruns `check-recoverable-safe-recovery-target.mjs` immediately
before transaction visibility checks and publication. Its canonical Safe-state
projection must be byte-identical to the observation used to prepare that role;
only the finalized observation block number and hash may advance.
The target checker covers Ethereum chain ID, Safe address/runtime/singleton,
owners, threshold, nonce, modules, guard, transaction hash and the cataloged RPC
consensus. Any concurrent substitution or replay-state change fails closed.

Adversarial validation

The focused operator-runbook test requires the prepublication target document
and its exact comparison to occur after the existing local source/evidence
checks and before the only recovery `publish` command. Moving the observation
earlier across signing/review, deleting it or publishing first fails the test.

No Cargo, Foundry, Anvil, full readiness, proof, network, signing, funds,
deployment or publication operation was run.
