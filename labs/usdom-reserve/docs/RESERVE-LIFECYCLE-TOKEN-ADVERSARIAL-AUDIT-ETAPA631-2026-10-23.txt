Stage 631 — reserve lifecycle and hostile-token audit (2026-10-23)

Scope

Audited the remaining Solidity lifecycle invariants across the proof reserve
and quorum prototype: deposit and burn identifier consumption, replay/collision
domains, token callbacks and nonstandard USDT returns, fee/blacklist policy,
pause/rotation/recovery, and conservation of credited collateral. Temporal and
arithmetic boundaries already covered in stage 628 were not repeated.

Result

No additional contract defect was confirmed.

- Deposit identifiers bind chain, reserve, funder and salt. Any transfer
  failure, callback failure or exact-delta mismatch reverts the complete
  transaction and leaves the identifier reusable.
- Burn/request identifiers are consumed only after an accepted proof/quorum.
  Failed proof, callback, token policy or transfer paths cannot mark them paid,
  create a permanent proof binding or consume rate capacity.
- All value paths are nonreentrant. A token callback cannot enter deposit,
  queue, execute or discard while an outer transfer is active. Effects-first
  payout state rolls back atomically when transfer/postconditions fail.
- No-return USDT semantics are supported by `SafeERC20`, but success still
  requires exact sender, reserve and recipient balance deltas. Fee-on-top,
  fee-on-receive, lying, false or malformed behavior cannot mint deposit credit
  or finalize a payout.
- Blacklist, pause, deprecation or configured fee policy fails before value
  movement. If policy changes after queueing, the exact claim and bucket remain
  retryable after recovery rather than being silently paid or erased.
- Donations never increase credited outstanding supply. Confiscation below
  outstanding credit blocks admissions and payments until collateral returns.
- Emergency pause invalidates the current proof queue in O(1). In the quorum
  prototype, emergency recovery revokes active keys and requires a fresh epoch
  plus delayed, configuration-bound resume; ordinary rotation invalidates old
  certificates without granting a fund-moving admin path.

Material regressions added

1. `testProofReserveReentrancyCannotConsumeDepositOrBurnIdentifiers` makes the
   collateral token call back into deposit during `transferFrom`, then into a
   proved queue admission during payout. Both nested operations fail while the
   outer exact transfer succeeds; no callback deposit, pending request or
   permanent proof binding is created, and the valid burn queues normally once
   the callback is removed.
2. `testProofReserveFeeModesRollbackAndNoReturnUSDTStyleRemainsExact` exercises
   fee-on-top and fee-on-receive deposits and payouts, checking rollback of
   balances, credit, reservations, paid flags and bucket capacity. It then
   proves exact no-return deposits and payouts remain usable.
