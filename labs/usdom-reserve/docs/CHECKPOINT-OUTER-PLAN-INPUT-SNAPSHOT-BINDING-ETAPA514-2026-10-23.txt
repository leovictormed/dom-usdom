USD0M stage 514 — checkpoint outer-plan input snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The checkpoint outer planner consumed the complete embedded segment-plan input
surface plus live segment job and receipt indexes, segment receipts and outer
guest artifacts only after Cargo compilation. Replacement could make its three
outputs describe multiple filesystem generations.

Correction
==========

The planner now captures all five upstream indexes, every canonical segment
source, referenced RandomX/economic receipt, the segment ELF/program/ImageID,
the segment job and receipt indexes, every uniquely cataloged canonical segment
receipt, and the outer ELF/program/ImageID into one owner-only layout replica.
Planning runs only inside that replica. The statement and chain are durably
installed before the outer job index, so interruption leaves no completion
marker for a partially published plan.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook suite asserts the full private replica, strict receipt
catalog, isolated execution and publication order. Readiness requires
`production_checkpoint_outer_plan_inputs_snapshotted=true`. No proof, network,
signing, deployment or funds operation is executed.
