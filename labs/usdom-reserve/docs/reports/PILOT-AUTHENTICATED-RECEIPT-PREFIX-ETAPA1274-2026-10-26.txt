Stage 1274 — authenticated pilot receipt-prefix recovery
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
A partial deposit receipt list was classified as resumable before its nonce,
canonical-chain result and two-source finality count were checked. Prepared and
partial states also did not universally bind the observed pending nonce to the
base nonce plus the number of known transactions. A foreign transaction or
reorged partial receipt could therefore be described as safe to resume.

Correction
----------
Every existing deposit and redemption receipt now forms an exact three-field,
ordered prefix. Each nonce must equal base plus its index. A receipt is either
canonical with exactly two finalized sources, or pending recheck with canonical
null and zero finalized sources. The pending account nonce must equal base plus
prefix length before any phase status is returned, including an empty prefix.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Regressions reject a foreign first nonce, gaps, injected receipt fields,
canonical false, incomplete finalized quorum, inconsistent pending markers and
pending nonces consumed beyond the authenticated prefix. A well-formed pending
receipt remains classified as finality-recheck-required.
