Stage 1188 — EIP-1271 collector bounded quantities
Date: 2026-10-26

The common Ethereum quantity helpers validated canonical syntax before BigInt
but did not bound lexical length first. The finalized EIP-1271 collector also
parsed raw RPC chain, block and timestamp quantities directly. Decimal and hex
helpers now derive a lexical ceiling from the configured maximum before
conversion. The collector applies uint256 to chain ID, uint64 to finalized
height/timestamp, and requires an exact bytes32 finalized hash.

Adversarial tests reject 100,000-digit decimal and hex quantities before
BigInt. Quantity, native-attestation and release-authorization suites pass
18/18 with pinned Node 24.19.0; targeted diff-check passes.
