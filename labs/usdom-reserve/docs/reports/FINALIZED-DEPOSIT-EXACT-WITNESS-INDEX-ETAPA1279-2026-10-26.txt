Stage 1279 — exact finalized-deposit witness-index authority
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The finalized-deposit witness index was checked field by field but did not
require its writer-defined schema to be exact. The terminal pilot conservation
check consumed an even smaller subset. Ambiguous extensions or malformed
unconsumed witness metadata could therefore cross the issuance-to-payout
evidence boundary.

Correction
----------
A shared index validator now requires exactly twelve fields and validates the
canonical manifest/witness hashes, fixed witness filename, positive safe-integer
size capped at 4 MiB, execution and no-broadcast flags, exact one-USDT amount,
lowercase deposit ID and a nonzero BN254-field receiving commitment. Full native
issuance and terminal financial conservation both invoke this authority.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Financial-conservation regressions reject injected and missing index fields,
zero witness length and a length above 4 MiB. Existing issuance substitutions,
witness-byte identity and registration bindings remain covered.
