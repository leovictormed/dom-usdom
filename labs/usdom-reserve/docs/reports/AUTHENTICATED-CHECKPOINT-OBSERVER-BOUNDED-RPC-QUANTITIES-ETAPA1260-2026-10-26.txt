Stage 1260 — bounded RPC quantities in authenticated checkpoint observation
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The authenticated checkpoint observer applied permissive hexadecimal regexes
and then converted RPC chain ID, finalized block number and timestamp directly
with `BigInt`. A bounded HTTP response could still contain a very large numeric
string, and noncanonical leading-zero or uppercase quantity spellings reached
the authority path before the candidate observation validator.

Correction
----------
The collector now canonicalizes the raw chain ID as a bounded uint256 Ethereum
quantity and requires exact mainnet value 1. Finalized block number and timestamp
use the shared canonical uint64 quantity reader before the block tag is used or
the observation is formed. The former direct conversions are absent. Hash,
snapshot stability, registry runtime/state and multisource agreement checks
remain intact.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
The live-hardening regression proves all three raw channels pass through the
bounded readers and that direct finalized-number/timestamp conversion is absent.
The shared quantity suite exercises 100,000-digit, overflow, leading-zero,
uppercase and malformed inputs; checkpoint observation regressions independently
reject out-of-range finalized evidence.

- Targeted diff check: pass.
