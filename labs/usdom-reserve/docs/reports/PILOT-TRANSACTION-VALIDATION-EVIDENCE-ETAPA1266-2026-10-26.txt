Stage 1266 — exact and bounded pilot transaction-validation evidence
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The closed-mainnet phase reconciler trusted selected fields from deposit and
redemption raw-validation documents, but did not require the writer-defined
field set or independently bound the persisted nonce and signed gas envelope.
An altered validation could therefore carry ambiguous extra fields, substitute
deposit token/reserve metadata, or send an oversized nonce into later BigInt
arithmetic before the DAG rejected it.

Correction
----------
Both validation schemas are now exact. Nonce is canonical uint64 text before
conversion. Gas limit, maximum fee, priority fee and maximum cost are canonical
bounded uint256 text, their product is exact, and the existing phase-specific
gas, fee and transaction-cost ceilings are independently applied. Deposit
validation token and reserve are rebound to the exact deposit package before a
receipt contributes to phase state.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Tests reject leading-zero, 100,000-digit and uint64-overflow nonces, injected
fields, token/reserve substitutions, zero or excessive gas/fee, priority above
maximum fee, and forged maximum cost. A separate redemption fixture proves its
narrower schema and independent phase limits.
