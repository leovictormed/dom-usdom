Stage 1455 — TPM keystore provisioning bounded cleanup
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
The TPM keystore provisioner pinned Node, but its EXIT/signal cleanup called the Node wrapper without a deadline. A wedged deletion could retain recovery and TPM owner-auth material indefinitely and prevent the shell from completing signal handling.

Correction
The cleanup reattests the pinned Node tool, executes recursive removal through the authenticated timeout with TERM at 30 seconds and KILL five seconds later, discards child output, reattests Node again, and requires the private directory to be absent. Timeout, identity drift, deletion failure or residual presence forces a failing final status.
