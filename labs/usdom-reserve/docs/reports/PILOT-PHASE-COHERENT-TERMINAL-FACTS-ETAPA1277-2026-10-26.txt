Stage 1277 — phase-coherent pilot terminal facts
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
Terminal financial fields were not uniformly constrained before terminal phase
completion. A prepared or partial deposit could carry residual allowance, and
a prepared redemption or one awaiting receipt finality could claim that burn
and request had already been paid. These contradictory snapshots weakened
crash-recovery classification.

Correction
----------
Deposit allowance must be exactly zero in every phase. Redemption paidBurn and
paidRequest must both remain false unless exactly two sequential receipts are
canonical and finalized by two sources. Existing terminal reconciliation still
requires both paid facts to be true after both receipts finalize.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Regressions reject residual allowance before any deposit receipt, paid facts
before any redemption receipt, and paid facts while the execute receipt awaits
finality. The equivalent pending-finality state with both paid facts false
remains classified as finality-recheck-required.
