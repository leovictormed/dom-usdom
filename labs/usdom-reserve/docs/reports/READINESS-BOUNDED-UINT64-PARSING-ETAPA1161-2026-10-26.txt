Stage 1161 — readiness bounded uint64 parsing
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
The release-readiness consumer accepted arbitrary-length decimal strings before
converting attestation, finalized-snapshot and authorization timestamps through
BigInt/EIP-712 helpers. Although later numeric checks rejected values above
uint64, an attacker-controlled evidence document could force avoidable parsing
of a very large integer first.

Change
- Added one canonical nonzero uint64 predicate with a 20-digit lexical ceiling
  and an exact uint64 numeric ceiling.
- Applied it to native-attestation completion time, both finalized approval
  snapshots and residual-risk/release-authorization acceptance time.
- EIP-712 digest construction and timestamp ordering now run only after the
  bounded timestamp predicate succeeds.
- Added 100,000-digit negative vectors for each affected evidence class.
