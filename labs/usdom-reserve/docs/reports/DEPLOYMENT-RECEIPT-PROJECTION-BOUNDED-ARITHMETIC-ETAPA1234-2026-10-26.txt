Stage 1234 — bounded RPC receipt arithmetic before deployment projection
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
`projectDeploymentReceipt` multiplied raw RPC `gasUsed` and
`effectiveGasPrice` values with `BigInt` before the projected receipt reached
the strict evidence validator. A malicious or faulty RPC could therefore force
conversion and multiplication of an arbitrarily large hexadecimal integer even
though the resulting evidence would later be rejected.

Correction
----------
The projector now canonicalizes and bounds both quantities before arithmetic.
Gas used is capped by the deployment gas policy and effective gas price by the
deployment fee policy. The canonical RPC strings remain in the evidence while
the already bounded decimal values are used to calculate actual cost. The final
closed-schema validator and cost relation remain unchanged.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Regressions prove fail-closed behavior for 100,000-hex-digit gas and fee values,
and for each exact policy maximum plus one. All fail before cost multiplication.

Validation
----------

  27/27 pass.
- Targeted diff check: pass.

Scope
-----

signature, deployment, funds, commit or push operation was performed.
