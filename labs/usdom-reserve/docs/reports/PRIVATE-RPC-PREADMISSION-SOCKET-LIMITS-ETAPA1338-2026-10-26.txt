Finding
=======

The proxy limited active application requests to eight, but that counter starts
only after complete headers, canonical framing and capability routing. An
unbounded number of loopback sockets could remain in slow-header or idle states
outside the counter, consuming descriptors until the coarse timeout sweep.

Correction
==========

The HTTP server now caps established connections at 16 and drops excess
connections. Timeout enforcement is checked every second. Idle sockets are
destroyed after 15 seconds, while the existing 10-second header deadline,
125-second whole-request deadline, one-second keepalive and one-request-per-
socket policy remain active. The application-level eight-request and per-source
quotas continue to apply after admission.
