Stage 1257 — independently validated Safe deployment receipt authority
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The Safe deployment receipt checker trusted a private raw-validation journal
after checking only its schema and role, then consumed its transaction hash and
sender. It did not require the journal's exact schema or independently bind its
Safe, factory target, nonce, calldata/raw hashes, gas and fee bounds. A modified
journal could therefore retain the two consumed fields while becoming
ambiguous or inconsistent with the package.

Correction
----------
Receipt validation now requires the exact raw-journal field set and independently
checks the role Safe and target against the package, nonce against the safe
integer domain, nonzero bounded gas and fee, and all three nonzero hashes before
binding the RPC receipt. The raw transaction validator also explicitly rejects
zero max fee or priority fee above max fee in addition to its fixed priority
policy.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Receipt regressions mutate extra/missing fields, Safe, target, nonce spelling,
gas ceiling, fee ceiling and zero raw hash. All fail before receipt projection.

----------

- Safe deployment receipt, finality, raw transaction, executor and deployment
  suites: 12/12 pass.
- Targeted diff check: pass.
