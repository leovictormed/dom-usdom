USDOM mainnet readiness — Stage 1334
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The private RPC proxy enforced byte ceilings while accumulating request and
upstream response streams, but did not reject an oversized declared
Content-Length before reading, explicitly terminate an oversized stream, or
clear the intermediate chunks after Buffer.concat. A hostile local client or
provider could continue consuming connection/memory resources after the gate had
decided to reject the body, while raw-transaction chunks remained until GC.

Correction
==========

Request and upstream Content-Length values are now parsed canonically and
rejected when malformed or above their respective 4 MiB and 32 MiB limits.
Chunked streams retain the measured ceiling. On overflow, Node request streams
are destroyed and async iterators are closed, which cancels upstream Web-stream
consumption. Every accumulated chunk is zeroed in finally after Buffer.concat or
on rejection. The combined request/response cleanup from Stage 1332 remains in
place.

- Lifecycle assertions require both declared-size gates, Node-stream destruction,
