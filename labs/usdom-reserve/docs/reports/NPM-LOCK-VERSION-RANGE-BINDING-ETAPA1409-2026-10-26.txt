Stage 1409 — npm lock version-range binding
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
Dependency edges resolved to an installed lock entry, but their requirement was not checked
against that entry's version. An otherwise valid lock could connect `^1` to a checksummed 2.x
archive and still satisfy the previous existence-only closure check.

Correction
- Parse the exact requirement forms present in the reviewed locks: exact/partial, caret and tilde
  semantic versions, including prerelease exclusion.
- Resolve each dependency by nearest-ancestor node_modules, retrieve that exact entry and require
  its version to satisfy the declaring edge's range.
- Reject unsupported requirement syntax or malformed installed versions rather than guessing.
- Implement npm caret rules for major-zero compatibility and tilde minor boundaries.
- Adversarial regressions cover major substitution, zero-major caret escape, tilde escape,
  prerelease substitution and a lock edge whose installed version falls outside its range.
