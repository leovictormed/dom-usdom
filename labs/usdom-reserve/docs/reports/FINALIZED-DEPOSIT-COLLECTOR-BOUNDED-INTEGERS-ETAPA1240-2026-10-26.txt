Stage 1240 — bounded external integers in the finalized-deposit collector
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
Although the completed finalized-deposit artifact is validated, the live
collector converted raw execution quantities and Beacon decimal slots/blocks
with `BigInt` while it was still acquiring ancestry. Oversized inputs could
therefore consume resources before artifact validation. Mixed-case and
leading-zero Ethereum quantities were also accepted during collection.

Correction
----------
The collector now routes execution quantities through the shared canonical
uint64 Ethereum-quantity reader and Beacon slots/block positions through the
shared canonical decimal uint64 reader before conversion. The bounded values
drive period calculations, finalized ancestry loops, receipt ordering and block
identity checks. The final artifact validator remains an independent barrier.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
A collector-specific regression proves every raw external conversion site uses
the bounded readers and forbids the former direct conversions. Behavioral cases
reject 100,000-digit values, exact 2^64, leading-zero quantities/decimals,
uppercase quantities and negative decimals before conversion.
