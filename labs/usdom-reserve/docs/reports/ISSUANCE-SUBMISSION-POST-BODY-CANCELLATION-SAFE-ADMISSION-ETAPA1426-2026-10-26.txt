Issuance submission post-body, cancellation-safe admission
Date: 2026-10-26

Scope
- Authenticated POST /usdom/v4/issuance/submit/v1
- Binary witness delivery, finality-verification admission, client cancellation, bounded memory and queueing

Finding (material availability defect)
The route's ConcurrencyLimitLayer(1) wrapped the entire HTTP request, including delivery of a witness body up to 4 MiB. An authenticated slow sender could occupy the only finality-verification slot without completing a witness. Once a node submission began, cancellation of the HTTP future could also release route admission while detached node/blocking work continued, allowing overlapping expensive jobs to accumulate behind internal locks.

Correction
- Added a two-permit body-delivery admission distinct from finality verification.
- The handler acquires the one-permit finality worker only after the bounded body is complete and media/empty checks pass.
- Moved the owned worker permit into a spawned Tokio task containing the node submission. Disconnect/timeout no longer releases it before node work ends.
- Excess complete witnesses fail immediately with retryable HTTP 503 instead of queueing.
- The existing 4 MiB body cap, 120-second route timeout, bearer authentication and rate limit remain in force.
