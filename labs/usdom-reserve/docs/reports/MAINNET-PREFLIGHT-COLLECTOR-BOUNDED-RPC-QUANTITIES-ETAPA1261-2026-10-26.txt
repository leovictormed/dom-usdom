Stage 1261 — bounded RPC quantities in the mainnet preflight collector
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The production preflight collector converted `eth_chainId` directly with
`BigInt` and converted the finalized block again when building its observation.
Snapshot pinning later bounded the block, and time consensus later bounded the
timestamp, but the collector did not establish all three numeric authorities at
the RPC ingress before using the finalized tag for its broad contract reads.

Correction
----------
The collector now canonicalizes chain ID as a bounded uint256 Ethereum quantity
and requires exact mainnet 1. It canonicalizes finalized block number and
timestamp as uint64 immediately after the concurrent RPC response and before
snapshot pinning or contract access. The already canonical decimal block value
is placed in the observation; former direct conversions are absent.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Static collector regression proves the three bounded-reader calls and absence
of the two direct conversion patterns. Shared quantity and transport regressions
exercise oversized, overflow, leading-zero, uppercase and malformed responses.

  suites: 45/45 pass.
- Targeted diff check: pass.

Scope
