Stage 1285 — exact DOM issuance RPC authorities
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The DOM mainnet identity and issuance admission response validators checked all
consumed values but accepted unknown fields. An RPC response with conflicting
or future-shaped authority data could therefore pass subset validation before
the private issuance intent or durable restart receipt was recorded.

Correction
----------
DOM identity now requires exactly the nine fields emitted by the pinned RPC API.
Issuance admission requires exactly schema_version, accepted, candidate_id and
state. Existing positive safe-integer protocol/tip/scan constraints, mainnet
network and magic, nonzero chain/genesis/tip hashes, and nonzero queued candidate
requirements remain unchanged.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Regressions reject extra and missing fields in both response types, alongside
the existing regtest identity, network-magic substitution and zero candidate
identifier cases.

----------

- Issuance, conservation, deposit/payout evidence, pilot DAG, wallet receipt
  and finalized-source suites: 21/21 pass.
- Targeted diff check: pass.

Scope
-----

Ledger, signature, deployment, funds, commit or push operation was performed.
