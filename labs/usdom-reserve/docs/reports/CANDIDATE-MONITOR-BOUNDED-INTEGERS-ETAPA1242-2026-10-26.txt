Stage 1242 — bounded integer parsing in the candidate monitor
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
-------
The candidate monitor required canonical decimal spelling for private-chain
views and persisted monitor state, but converted arbitrary-length strings with
`BigInt`. An authenticated yet malformed snapshot could therefore force an
oversized conversion before the monitor reached its tighter height, amount or
confirmation policy checks.

Correction
----------
The monitor's common integer reader now rejects more than 78 decimal digits
before conversion and independently enforces the uint256 ceiling afterwards.
All view height, checkpoint height, burn amount/height/confirmations and durable
state channels that use this reader inherit the bound. Their existing narrower
semantic checks remain in force.

Adversarial regression
----------------------
Regressions inject a 100,000-digit height and burn amount plus exact 2^256 in
height and confirmations. Each fails at the bounded integer authority before
array indexing or downstream state logic.

- Targeted diff check: pass.
