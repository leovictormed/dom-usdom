first-deposit package were written through predictable `/tmp` paths. The latter

  Revalidation must produce byte-identical public identity.

  generation, then installed create-only in the private control-plane
  directory. It cannot be silently regenerated with another salt.
- Both persistent artifacts are consumed through the strict direct private-file
  reader. Runbook regression rejects the former paths.
