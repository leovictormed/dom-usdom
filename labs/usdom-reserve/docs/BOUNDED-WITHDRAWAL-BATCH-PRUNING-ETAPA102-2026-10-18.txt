Bounded withdrawal batch pruning — etapa 102
Date: 2026-10-18
Status: production reserve candidate hardened; production verifier and release remain unconfigured

Superseded queue-lifetime detail (stage 210): production queue expiry is now a
fixed renewable lease measured from proof admission. The irreversible native
burn's validUntil no longer extinguishes its payout right. pruneDiscardable
remains necessary for O(1) emergency-generation invalidation and bounded cleanup.

Finding USDOM-102

Severity: Medium operational availability and storage-cleanup risk.
Current public exposure: none; no commit, push, deployment or transaction was performed.

The proof reserve linked pending withdrawals by admission order, while each
certificate selected its own validUntil inside the allowed window. A later
entry could therefore expire before a still-valid head. pruneExpired correctly
stopped at that head, leaving middle and tail entries removable only through
one discardWithdrawal transaction per identifier. Emergency pause also
invalidated the whole queue in O(1) but deliberately left each old mapping for
individual cleanup. A large reviewed queue could make incident recovery and
storage reclamation unnecessarily expensive.

Remediation

USDOMProofReserve now exposes pruneDiscardable(bytes32[]), which:

- accepts at most MAX_PRUNE_BATCH (256) candidates per transaction;
- removes an active entry only after its exact expiry;
- removes entries from queue generations invalidated by emergency pause;
- skips unknown, duplicate and currently valid identifiers instead of allowing
  one candidate to revert independent cleanup;
- uses the existing linked-list removal primitive so head, tail, neighboring
  pointers, pendingCount, totalPendingAmount and pendingRequests remain
  consistent;
- never clears paid burn or request replay protection;
- remains permissionless and nonReentrant and grants no payout authority.

The original prefix-oriented pruneExpired remains available for cheap ordered
cleanup. Relayers can use event-indexed burn identifiers with the bounded batch
path when expiry order differs from admission order or after an emergency.
