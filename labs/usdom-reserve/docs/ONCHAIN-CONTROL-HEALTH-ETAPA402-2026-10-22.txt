USDOM stage 402 — on-chain control health parity
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The finalized preflight independently reconstructed the control-plane checks,
but the deployed contracts did not expose their own evaluation. Operational
monitoring could therefore verify equivalent inputs without directly proving
that the deployed runtime evaluated its internal predicate as healthy.

Correction
==========

USDOMProofReserve now exposes controlPlaneHealthy(); DOMCheckpointRegistry
exposes guardianControlHealthy(). Each view delegates to the exact private
predicate already used by reserve value/resume paths or irreversible checkpoint
halt. No weaker duplicate policy implementation was introduced.

The post-deployment preflight reads both getters at its fixed finalized block
and requires true in addition to independently checking proxy/singleton code,
threshold, owners, modules and terminal cursor. A contract/policy discrepancy
therefore fails the cold-start gate even if the external reconstruction appears
valid.

Evidence
========

- Focused policy/getter and preflight tests pass.
- Complete Solidity suite: 145/145 pass, including invariant campaigns.
- Complete Node suite: 304/304 pass.
- Current deployed runtime sizes are 16,141 bytes for the reserve and 7,262
  bytes for the checkpoint registry, below the 24,576-byte EIP-170 limit.
- Production lint/forced build, imported provenance and whitespace checks pass.

No live RPC, signature, transaction, deployment, funds, mining, project commit,
tag or push occurred. The detached finalized-burn capacity proof remained
active and was not restarted or modified.
