Stage 548 — DOM burn to Ethereum redemption static audit
Date: 2026-10-23

Result

No distinct exploitable replay, substitution, timing, queue or USDT accounting
defect was established across burn intent, finalized source, proof packaging,
submission, queue and execution. No implementation or readiness fact changed.

Binding and replay evidence

- The native intent and finalized statement retain burn ID, request ID,
  recipient, amount, DOM domain, validity deadline, finalized height/block/state,
  Beacon slot, Ethereum chain, reserve and activation schedule commitment.
- Source, receipt and Groth16 indexes transitively bind exact input, statement,
  ImageID, journal digest, verifier parameters and seal hashes. Submission
  reconstructs calldata and rejects any field, proof, selector, hash, chain or
  reserve substitution. Inputs are owner-only snapshotted and origins rechecked.
- Queue admission rejects paid burn IDs, paid request IDs, active burn entries
  and active request collisions. Execution recomputes the complete statement
  hash. Successful payout permanently records both replay keys.

Timing, finality and queue evidence

- Admission requires a nonfuture Beacon slot within the seven-day finality
  window. The native validity deadline cannot exceed the independently computed
  queue lease. Execution requires the six-hour delay, rejects expired leases
  and rejects any queue generation invalidated by pause.
- Pause invalidates the queue generation and aggregate reservations in O(1).
  Stale entries cannot execute; bounded or targeted cleanup releases their
  storage, and the irreversible native debt may be proved again.
- Statement and proof verification happen before reservation. Available
  collateral, outstanding exposure, per-burn capacity and the token bucket are
  checked before queue or payout effects.

USDT and TOCTOU evidence

- Token runtime identity and paused/deprecated/fee/frozen policy are rechecked
  on value paths. Deposit and payout require exact reserve and counterparty
  balance deltas, rejecting fee-on-transfer or changed semantics atomically.
- Queue and execute are nonreentrant. Execution applies replay, accounting and
  bucket effects before transfer, with total transaction rollback on failure.
- Unsigned submission packages set broadcast authorization false and can be
  checked against live chain ID, reserve code and on-chain statement hashing.
  Proof packaging and submission preparation use private replicas, exact hashes,
  source rereads and output-last durable publication.

Validation

Focused Node submission and native burn-identifier tests passed. Lightweight
source assertions confirmed dual replay keys, statement-hash execution binding,
delay/expiry/epoch checks, exact token deltas and input-origin rechecks. Diff
whitespace checks passed.

No full readiness, Cargo, Foundry, Anvil, proof, network, signing, funds,
deployment, push or publication operation was run.
