USDⓄM RESERVE — EXPECTED BLOCKER DIAGNOSTIC (STAGE 422)
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding

The early closed-mainnet diagnostic runs under `set -euo pipefail`, while its
correct result before adapter deployment is exit status 1 with a nonempty
blocker list. The previous pipeline therefore could terminate the shell before
displaying the blockers. It was also the final predictable `/tmp` pathname in
the mainnet runbook.

The release-signing identity pipeline could likewise consume a partial producer
stream and was not idempotent even though revalidation should return the exact
same public identity.

Correction

- The diagnostic now writes to a fresh 0600 temporary file, explicitly requires
  exit status 1, validates `ready == false` with a positive blocker count,
  displays the blockers and removes the file.
- Any unexpected status, including premature success or internal error, fails
  the phase.
- Release-signing identity output is staged only after a successful command and
  installed through the immutable/idempotent private writer. Different bytes
  are rejected; exact revalidation succeeds.
- The runbook now contains no predictable `/tmp` path.
