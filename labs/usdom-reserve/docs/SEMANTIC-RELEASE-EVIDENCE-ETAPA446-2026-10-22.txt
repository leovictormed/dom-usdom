USD0M stage 446 - semantic release evidence

Security validation and operations review are no longer accepted solely by
path and SHA-256. Canonical validators bind their contents to the exact
release, source commit and tag, requirements/source manifest or operator
runbook, immutable contract identities and the required independent evidence.

The security writer executes the fixed production lint/build, Node, Solidity,
deep fuzz/invariant, provenance and whitespace checks. The operations review
binds nine operational evidence classes and exact fail-closed assertions.
Both writers use private directories, locks, create-once installation, input
rechecks and durable file/directory synchronization.

These reports provide objective machine evidence. They do not create release
authorization, custody approval, deployment permission or real-funds consent.
