ETAPA731 — current deployment finalized evidence manifest
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

ETAPA728 hardened recovery evidence, but the current deployment path still
persisted raw validation and a provider receipt as separate leaves. There was
no single create-once record proving that the exact signed role transaction had
reached a canonical finalized block with the approved runtime. Recovery could
reconstruct that graph, but initial production and recovery did not share one
completion state.

Correction
==========

After cross-RPC receipt agreement, canonical/finalized ancestry and runtime
agreement, the executor now derives
`contract-deployment-N-evidence.json`. Its exact v1 schema binds:

- role index/name and Ethereum chain ID;
- release, package, RPC catalog and identity-observation commitments;
- signed raw SHA-256, transaction hash, sender and nonce;
- initcode hash, predicted CREATE address and approved runtime hash; and
- canonical finalized receipt block number and block hash.

The manifest is installed create-once with mode 0600 and an identical rerun is
the only accepted reuse. It is emitted only after the primary receipt evidence
is durable. Recovery for every later role requires this manifest, strictly
parses it, reconciles every field against package/validation/receipt evidence,
and byte-rechecks it after RPC observation. Therefore a next role cannot treat
a published, pending, failed, nonfinalized or incompletely recorded prior role
as complete.

Evidence
========

- Structural regression asserts manifest schema, path, context, receipt block
  binding and recovery snapshot/recheck.
- Focused Node tests: 19/19 passed.
- Bash syntax and `git diff --check`: passed.

No RPC call, materialization, signing, transmission, deployment, funds action,
commit or push was performed.
