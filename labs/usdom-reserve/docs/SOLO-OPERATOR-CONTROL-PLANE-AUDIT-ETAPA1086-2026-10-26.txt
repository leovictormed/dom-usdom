+USDOM Stage 1086 — closed-mainnet solo-operator control-plane audit
Date: 2026-10-26
Scope: static and lightweight Node/bash review; no network, deployment, Forge or Cargo

Result
------
The deployed contracts and release preflight correctly require 2-of-3 Safe
control. A 1-of-1 Safe cannot satisfy USDOMProofReserve's immutable
CONTROL_PLANE_THRESHOLD/OWNER_COUNT policy and remains rejected. No EOA may be
substituted directly because each control address, singleton code identity,
owner commitment and Safe policy is checked.

The pilot may therefore be operated by one person only by controlling two
separate owner keys while a third independent recovery key exists. This is
single-person operational control, not multisig independence, and must remain
limited to the fixed 1 USDT pilot exposure/capacity and migrate before limits
increase.

Verified controls
-----------------
- governance, reserve guardian and checkpoint guardian are separate Safe roles;
- release preflight now explicitly rejects reuse of one Safe address across roles;
- each Safe requires threshold 2, three distinct nonzero code-free EOA owners;
- Safe singleton/runtime, fallback handler, no modules, no guard and module
  sentinel are pinned at one finalized dual-RPC snapshot;
- Safe setup binds chain 1 deployment identity, distinct CREATE2 salt nonces and
  zero delegatecall/payment fields;
- signing/recovery code binds EIP-712 chain/domain/verifying Safe, Safe nonce,
  transaction payload and finalized snapshots;
- recovery changes one owner through signed Safe transactions and has a path
  back to independently controlled owners.

Safe v2 closure
---------------
The shared owner trio described by the original Stage 1086 review has been
removed. The production schema now requires three independent owner sets with
nine globally distinct addresses. Deployment, recovery, evidence,
materialization, authorization, signing, readiness and preflight reject the v1
shared-trio format fail-closed. Device and seed independence remains an
operational fact that must be attested without disclosing secrets.
