USD0M stage 530 — finalized-burn capacity source-receipt snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The finalized-burn capacity runner selected a successful proof run early but
reopened its succinct receipt and receipt index only after regenerating the
capacity vector and plan. Replacement could combine receipt bytes, index and
source-run status from different generations.

Correction
==========

Immediately after fixing the source run, both bounded private files are copied
into an owner-only capacity-run replica. Each installed file is reread and
checked for exact byte and SHA-256 equality. The downstream proof layout is
populated only from captured buffers. After isolated EVM execution and before
report validation/publication, both origins are reread and must still match the
captured bytes and hashes.

Evidence and scope
==================

A direct adversarial test mutates the receipt index after capture and proves
the final origin check rejects it. Readiness requires
`production_finalized_burn_capacity_source_receipts_snapshotted_and_origins_rechecked=true`.
No Cargo, Foundry, proof, network, signing, deployment, funds operation, commit
or push was run. Full readiness remains deferred while RandomX proving is active.
