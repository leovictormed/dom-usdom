ETAPA1044 — closed-mainnet payout delta evidence

Concrete gap
------------
The redemption executor finalized queue/execute receipts and required paidBurns and
paidRequests, but its terminal evidence did not prove that the intended recipient's
USDT balance increased by exactly 1,000,000 base units. Boolean replay state alone
was insufficient operational evidence of the real-funds outcome.

Correction
----------
Before signing the execute transaction, the executor now authenticates the reserve's
token() against the release token on two RPCs, observes the recipient balance at the
finalized tag on both RPCs, requires equality, and durably installs a package-bound
mode-0600 baseline. Restart validates and reuses that exact baseline.

After the execute receipt is finalized with matching block/hash on both RPCs, each
RPC must report recipientBalance == baseline + 1,000,000. Terminal output-last
evidence binds the token, recipient, baseline SHA-256, before/after balances, exact
amount and dual-RPC finality. The offline checklist independently hashes and validates
the baseline and exact delta. Any donation or unrelated balance change during the
window fails closed and requires operator investigation rather than creating false
payout evidence.

Validation
----------
Pinned Node 24.19.0 focused payout and redemption tests passed 5/5. Shell syntax,
checker syntax and focused diff check passed. No network, signing, proof, deployment,
publication, push or funds were used.
