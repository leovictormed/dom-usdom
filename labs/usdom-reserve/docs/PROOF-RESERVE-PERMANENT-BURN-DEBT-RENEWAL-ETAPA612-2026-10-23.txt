Stage 612 — permanent finalized-burn debt renewal (2026-10-23)

Finding confirmed

`USDOMProofReserve` documented queue expiry as releasing only a temporary
collateral reservation while preserving the reserve's irreversible debt. The
implemented state did not preserve that promise:

- `discardWithdrawal` and pruning deleted both `pendingWithdrawals[burnId]` and
  `pendingRequests[requestId]` before payment;
- an exact requeue after the seven-day lease could fail
  `_requireRecentFinalitySlot`, because the statement's authenticated Beacon
  slot was now older than the same seven-day freshness window; and
- after expiry or emergency pause, another proved statement could reuse the
  old request with a different burn, or reuse the burn while changing public
  statement fields. An existing test explicitly renewed by replacing the
  original `finalityBeaconSlot`, which demonstrated the substitution.

The exact originally proved debt could therefore become unrecoverable while a
different statement was allowed to occupy its identifiers.

Correction

- Added permanent `provedBurnStatements[burnId]` and
  `provedRequestBurns[requestId]` bindings. They are written only after the
  first proof has passed the immutable verifier.
- First admission still requires a recent, nonfuture finalized Beacon slot and
  verifies the complete proof.
- Renewal may bypass only the slot-age admission check, and only when the full
  statement hash and burn/request pairing exactly match the permanent first
  admission. Recipient, amount, DOM block/state, finality slot, deadline and
  every other statement field remain immutable.
- Renewal still requires a correctly sized/selected proof and reruns the
  immutable verifier. An unapproved proof cannot renew an old debt.
- Queue expiry, pruning and emergency epoch invalidation continue to release
  temporary reserved collateral and linked-list storage, but never erase the
  permanent debt identity. Paid burn/request protections remain unchanged.

Tests

- Exact renewal succeeds after the original finality slot is older than the
  admission window and the temporary lease was discarded.
- A new burn using the old request is rejected after expiry and after pause.
- A changed finality slot, and therefore a changed statement, is rejected even
  with a separately accepted proof.
- Renewal with an unapproved proof is rejected.
- Emergency pause permits only the exact old debt to requeue after delayed
  resume; epoch-bound stale emergency protection remains intact.
- Queue invariants now require every active entry to match both permanent
  bindings.

Validation

- Focused expiry/pause renewal tests pass 3/3; the final strengthened renewal
  regression passes independently 1/1.
- `USDOMProofReserveTest` passes 40/40, including three 1,024-run fuzz tests.
- The full Solidity campaign passes 147/147 across 14 suites, including three
  256-run/16,384-call invariant campaigns.
- Production-contract lint and `git diff --check` pass. The installed formatter
  reports one unrelated pre-existing constructor layout difference; none of the
  changed sections is implicated.

No RISC Zero script, commit, push, deployment, signature or funds operation was
performed.
