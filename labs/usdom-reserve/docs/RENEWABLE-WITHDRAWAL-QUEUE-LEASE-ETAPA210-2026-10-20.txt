Stage 210 — renewable withdrawal queue lease after irreversible native burn
Date: 2026-10-20

Finding USDOM-210

Severity: High liveness / permanent user-value-loss risk.

The production reserve previously used the native burn's validUntil both as an
authenticated burn-intent field and as the final Ethereum execution deadline.
Native consensus permanently consumes the private notes and records their
nullifiers. There is no protocol transition that recreates those notes when an
Ethereum request expires. A pause, sustained congestion, rate-limit backlog,
temporary recipient blacklist or relayer outage lasting through validUntil
could therefore leave the USDT in the reserve after the matching USDOM had been
irreversibly destroyed.

Remediation

USDOMProofReserve now separates two clocks without weakening proof binding:

1. validUntil remains part of BurnIntent, burnId derivation and the finalized
   burn statement. The production proof must still show that native consensus
   admitted it under the authenticated Ethereum-anchor timing policy.
2. Proof admission rejects validUntil == 0 and any value still more than seven
   days in Ethereum's future. A historical deadline is accepted because an
   irreversible debt does not disappear when that historical time passes.
3. Each successful proof admission creates a fresh seven-day queue lease from
   the current Ethereum block timestamp. The fixed withdrawal delay, token
   policy checks, solvency checks and outflow bucket still apply.
4. Expired leases can be discarded permissionlessly. This releases only the
   pending collateral reservation and never marks burnId or requestId paid.
5. A fresh proof under a recent finalized Beacon slot can requeue the exact same
   unpaid burn and request. Successful payment permanently marks both identifiers,
   so renewal cannot authorize a duplicate payout.
6. Emergency queue invalidation has the same renewal behavior after delayed
   resume. Current-generation linked-list and reservation accounting remain exact.

Security properties retained

- recipient, amount, DOM state, reserve, chain and original intent remain bound
  by the same statement hash;
- proof admission still requires a recent finalized Ethereum Beacon slot;
- no governance, guardian, relayer or recipient can replace a payout field;
- no expired or invalidated queue entry consumes replay protection;
- no paid identifier can be cleared or requeued;
- rate limiting and the minimum execution delay restart on every renewal.

Residual boundary

USDT can still pause, deprecate, charge fees, blacklist accounts or destroy
blacklisted balances under its issuer-controlled policy. The reserve detects
those states and fails closed; it cannot guarantee USDT issuer availability.
That centralized-collateral risk belongs in the explicit residual-risk report.
It is not a demand for an impossible zero-risk guarantee.

No mining, proof generation, deployment, fund movement, commit or push was
performed.
