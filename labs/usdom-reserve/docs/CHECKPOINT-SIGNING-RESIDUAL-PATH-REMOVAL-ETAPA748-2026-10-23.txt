ETAPA 748 — checkpoint signing residual PATH authority removal
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
Checkpoint ratification retained two authority transforms outside its pinned tools: the snapshot
function invoked `node` through PATH, and final signature concatenation invoked `cut` through PATH.
Either replacement could change validated inputs or the bundle installed after Ledger signing.

Correction
==========
Snapshots now invoke the already pinned Node path. Signature halves are validated first and
concatenated with Bash's byte-stable ASCII substring operation, removing `cut` entirely. Negative
structural checks reject raw Node commands and external cut slicing.

Validation
==========
Signing and durable-writer suites pass 10/10. Shell syntax and diff checks pass. No ceremony,
Ledger, RPC or signing ran.

No commit, push, deploy, transmission or funds were used.
