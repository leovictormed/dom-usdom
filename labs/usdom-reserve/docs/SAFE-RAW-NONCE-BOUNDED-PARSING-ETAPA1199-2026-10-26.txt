SAFE RAW NONCE BOUNDED PARSING — STAGE 1199
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The strict Safe raw-transaction checker applied `BigInt` to a nonce of arbitrary
decimal length before enforcing `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`. A corrupted durable
validation artifact could therefore force costly parsing during resume or the
final evidence-graph barrier before being rejected.

Correction
==========

Nonce text must now be a canonical decimal string of no more than 16 digits
before `BigInt` conversion. The existing exact maximum-safe-integer bound and
transaction nonce equality remain cumulative. Regression coverage rejects
`2^53` and a 100,000-digit nonce.
