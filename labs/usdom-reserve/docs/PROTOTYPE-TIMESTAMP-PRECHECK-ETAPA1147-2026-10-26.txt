Stage 1147 — timestamp arithmetic precheck
Date: 2026-10-26

The quorum prototype added block.timestamp and a delay before checking whether
the result fit its canonical uint64 clock. At the uint256 boundary Solidity
could therefore emit Panic(0x11), bypassing the contract's explicit
TimestampOverflow boundary. _timestampAfter now validates both the delay and
the current timestamp against uint64.max before addition, matching the proof
reserve and preserving one deterministic fail-closed protocol error.

The timestamp regression now covers block.timestamp == uint256.max and passes
1/1 with pinned Forge and solc 0.8.37. The complete offline Forge suite also
recompiled the affected graph and exited without failures. Runtime is 14,610
bytes, leaving 9,966 bytes below EIP-170. git diff --check passes. No Cargo,
Rust, network, signing, deployment, funds, commit or push was used.
