ETAPA732 — final receipt/evidence reorg barrier
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding and correction
======================

The current deployment performed stable receipt and ancestry checks per RPC,
then reread local evidence before persistence. Source zero could still reorganize
while later sources were checked. The persisted completion manifest therefore
did not represent one final all-source observation immediately preceding its
create-once write.

A final barrier now runs after every earlier validation and immediately before
receipt/evidence persistence. It refetches the exact receipt from every RPC,
requires byte-normalized receipt agreement, rechecks the receipt block by
number and hash, and requires the receipt height at or below a single identical
finalized head number/hash reported by all sources. Approved runtime bytecode is
read at that exact finalized block. Raw, validation, submission-hash and RPC
catalog evidence are reread after the network sweep.

The create-once completion manifest now records that final barrier block number
and hash in addition to the receipt block. Any receipt drift, reorg, mixed
finalized head, runtime drift or local evidence mutation stops persistence.
