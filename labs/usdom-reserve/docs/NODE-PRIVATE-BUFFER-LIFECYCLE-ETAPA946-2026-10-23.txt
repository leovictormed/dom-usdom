ETAPA946 — NODE PRIVATE BUFFER LIFECYCLE
Date: 2026-10-23

Inventory
- Shared runtime-secret JSON reader serves 23 production scripts. Per read it
  owns exactly two credential-bearing Buffers: primary capture and independent
  stability verification. Both are erased in finally on success/error.
- Three secret-node launchers already track and fill their password/token
  buffers in finally. Raw/signature/witness readers are bounded artifact
  validators; JS strings created by JSON parsing cannot be reliably zeroized.

Correction
- The largest shared family retained its *_FILE handoff in process.env for the
  process lifetime and possible child inheritance. The helper now snapshots the
  path once and immediately deletes the environment entry before filesystem or
  JSON work. Both byte captures retain unconditional fill(0).
- Result: 23/23 consumers inherit the correction; two secret buffers per call
  erase on all exits and zero secret-file path variables remain inheritable.
