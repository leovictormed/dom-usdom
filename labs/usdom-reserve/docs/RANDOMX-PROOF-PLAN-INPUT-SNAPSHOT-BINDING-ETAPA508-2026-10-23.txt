USD0M stage 508 — RandomX proof-plan input snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The RandomX proof planner checked only the live statement-set index before
`cargo run`. Its host reopened that index, every statement and all three RISC
Zero guest artifacts after Cargo compilation, permitting a mixed input view.

Correction
==========

The wrapper captures the index, derives a bounded unique canonical statement
catalog, and captures every statement into an owner-only replica of the fixed
laboratory layout. It also copies ELF, packaged program and ImageID into that
replica; the host's existing hard-coded SHA-256 pins reject any copy-time
substitution. Planning runs from the replica. Its single canonical job index is
then captured and atomically installed at the production evidence path.

Evidence and scope
==================

The operator-runbook suite asserts private capture, replicated working root,
all three artifact names and durable publication. Readiness requires
`production_randomx_proof_plan_inputs_snapshotted=true`. No proof generation,
network access, signing, deployment or funds operation occurs in this stage.
