Stage 1396 — ownership transfer into candidate verification workers
Date: 2026-10-26

Finding

RPC candidate handlers already owned their decoded vectors. `NodeHandleImpl`
then borrowed those vectors when calling `DomNode`, and both v1 and v2 node
submission methods cloned all bytes solely to move them into `spawn_blocking`.
For candidate-v2 this duplicated up to 8 MiB of envelope plus 4 MiB of witness
bundle during admission. Issuance, transfer and burn v4 envelopes had the same
unnecessary clone pattern within their smaller route-specific limits.

Correction

The internal `DomNode` submission boundaries now accept owned vectors and move
them directly into the blocking verification closure. RPC node-handle paths
transfer the vectors they already own. The slice-based explicit Regtest helper
retains its single conversion because its caller does not transfer ownership.
Parsing, candidate identity, proof verification, pool accounting and retained
canonical envelope bytes are unchanged.

Memory/CPU review

- Issuance v4 and candidate v2 use binary HTTP bodies with route-specific caps;
  they do not incur base64 expansion. Private transfer/burn currently use hex
  JSON, but remain under the standard body ceiling and class-specific decoded
  payload limits.
- V2 framing copies its two components once out of Axum's immutable body into
  owned vectors needed across the async/node boundary. This stage removes the
  subsequent full-size node clone; Stage 1393 removed the candidate-ID
  concatenation allocation.
- Envelope/witness parsing copies the canonical payloads retained by the
  bounded pool. Expensive verification runs only after exact deduplication and
  pool count/aggregate-byte capacity checks.
- These are public proof, witness and commitment bytes, not wallet openings,
  seeds or signing keys. Mandatory zeroization would add full-buffer writes
  without protecting a secret lifecycle; wallet/prover secret types retain
  their separate zeroization rules.

Evidence

- `cargo check -p dom-node --features native-usdom-v4-poc` passes, compiling
  protocol, chain, RPC, wallet and node with the owned interfaces.
- `cargo test -p dom-rpc usdom_v2_candidate_submission --lib`: 2 passed,
  0 failed in 0.01s, covering authenticated exact binary submission and
  rejection of noncanonical framing.
- `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and `git diff --check` pass.
- No RandomX mining, external network, wallet, signature, deployment, funds,
  commit or push occurred.
