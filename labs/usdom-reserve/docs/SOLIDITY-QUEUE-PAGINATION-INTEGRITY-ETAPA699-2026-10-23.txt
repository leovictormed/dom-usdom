ETAPA 699 — Solidity queue pagination structural integrity
Date: 2026-10-23

Scope
-----
Adversarial review of USDOMProofReserve.pendingQueueSnapshot and
pendingBurnPage. This stage changed only Solidity and its tests. It did not
run Rust, Cargo, Node, RISC Zero, deployment, signing, transmission or funds.

Confirmed defect
----------------
The stage-694 getter bounded traversal to 256 records and rejected unknown or
old-generation cursors, but treated each record's `next` field as authoritative.
A corrupted cycle, broken reciprocal link, inconsistent head/tail, or count
that disagreed with a head traversal could therefore produce duplicated IDs,
an endless sequence of nonzero page cursors across calls, or a false completed
page. The individual call could not loop forever, but its output was not a
fail-closed structural view of the active queue.

Correction
----------
`pendingBurnPage` now snapshots and validates the queue header, including the
empty representation and positive aggregate for a nonempty queue. During its
strictly bounded traversal it requires:

* an active record from the requested queue generation;
* a zero predecessor at the head, or a live reciprocal predecessor for an
  explicit cursor;
* reciprocal previous/next links for every traversed edge;
* no repeated burn ID within the page;
* a zero next link only at the declared tail; and
* when traversal started at the head, agreement between the declared count
  and either the exact end or the first cursor beyond the requested page.

The maximum remains MAX_PRUNE_BATCH (256). Same-call mutation is impossible:
the view performs no external call. Cross-call mutations are detected by the
existing queue-epoch argument; clients must also pin snapshot and page calls
to one Ethereum block tag, as documented by pendingQueueSnapshot. An arbitrary
mid-list cursor cannot prove global reachability or total position in one
bounded page, but it must prove a live reciprocal incoming edge and every
subsequent page remains locally fail-closed.

Regression evidence
-------------------
Focused tests exercise valid head/middle/tail/end semantics, empty generation,
stale epoch/cursor, a cycle, a broken reciprocal edge, undersized and oversized
counts, missing head/tail/aggregate, and a real 256-entry page.
