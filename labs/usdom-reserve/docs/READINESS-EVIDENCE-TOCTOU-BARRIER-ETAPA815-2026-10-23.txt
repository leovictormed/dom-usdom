ETAPA 815 — aggregate readiness evidence TOCTOU barrier
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The readiness checker hashed and parsed each release evidence document from one
captured Buffer, but it did not reread those paths after its long-running child
checks. A document could be replaced after capture and before the final result;
the printed readiness decision would then authorize bytes no longer present at
the declared evidence path.

Correction

- Added a shared evidence-byte snapshot recheck that rereads every captured
  document and requires byte equality.
- The checker invokes the full recheck after all child outputs and pure
  assessment, immediately before printing the final result.
- Missing files, non-Buffer reads and any byte mutation fail closed.
- Forge/Git executable identity rechecks remain in place, so the final barrier
  follows both executable and child-output collection.

Validation

- Positive, mutation, disappearance and invalid-reread regressions pass.
- Full focused aggregate release/preflight/readiness suite: 81/81 pass.
- Diff check passes.
- No RPC, build, deployment, signature, commit, push or funds operation ran.
