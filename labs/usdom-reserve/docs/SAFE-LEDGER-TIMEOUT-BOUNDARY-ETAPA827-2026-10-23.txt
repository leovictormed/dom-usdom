ETAPA 827 — Safe Ledger timeout boundary
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

The recoverable-Safe signing ceremony strongly pinned Cast, Node, jq, cmp and
sha256sum, but its Ledger address and six signature calls had no deadline. A
stalled USB/device/provider interaction could retain the private ceremony,
terminal and temporary typed-data state indefinitely and prevent deterministic
cleanup.

Correction

- Added GNU timeout to the required, safely owned tool set and bound its resolved
  path, single-link metadata, owner/mode/directory, stat identity and SHA-256.
- Both Ledger command forms execute through that exact timeout binary with a
  five-minute deadline, TERM followed by KILL after five seconds, and foreground
  terminal semantics.
- Cast and timeout identities are checked immediately before and after every
  Ledger operation. Timeout or signal failure propagates through `set -e` into
  the existing status-preserving private cleanup.

Validation

- Bash syntax passes.
- Ledger timeout, tool-pinning, environment, descriptor, crash and cleanup
  suites: 17/17 pass.
- Diff check passes.
- No Ledger interaction, signature, RPC, deployment, commit, push or funds action ran.
