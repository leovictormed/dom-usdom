USD + DOM — reserve pause and upstream incident-response audit
Stage 556 — 2026-10-23

Scope
-----
Audited the production proof reserve's emergency pause generation, scheduled
resume cancellation and the RISC Zero upstream incident response planner and
executor. Tested stale queue generations, already-paused state, a pending
resume, single or disagreeing sources, forged source identities, malformed
observations, action ordering, receipt confirmation and final postconditions.

Result
------
No new exploitable defect was established.

- `pauseQueueEpoch(expectedQueueEpoch)` binds an automated action to the queue
  generation inspected. A stale action cannot invalidate a later reviewed
  queue.
- Calling it while already paused cancels `resumeReadyAt`; an incident cannot
  be bypassed by a previously scheduled governance resume.
- The executor recomputes the complete action plan, checks the chain, target
  addresses and runtime code, and rechecks queue epoch, paused state and resume
  timestamp before sending the reserve action.
- The reserve pause precedes the irreversible checkpoint halt. Unavailable,
  malformed, single-source or disagreeing upstream evidence can request the
  reversible reserve pause but cannot authorize `haltPermanently()`.
- A confirmed irreversible halt requires at least two catalog-bound sources
  with distinct operator identities and endpoint commitments, identical
  structurally authenticated finalized observations, successful inclusion and
  verified postconditions.
- The reserve and registry guardian roles remain independent, and a successful
  proposal is not treated as successful execution without a status-one receipt
  and on-chain state confirmation.

Validation
----------
A focused light selection from `test/node/risc0-incident-response.test.mjs`
passed 9/9. It covers healthy/no-action, confirmed incident ordering,
unconfirmed fail-safe pause, pending-resume cancellation, invalid stop state,
source disagreement and impersonation, already-paused execution, and
authenticated observation substitution. The deployment-style Anvil test was
intentionally excluded while the independent RandomX proof remains active.

No code or readiness claim changed. No Cargo, Foundry, public RPC, proof runner,
signing, deployment, funds, commit or push operation ran.
