ETAPA 816 — aggregate readiness authority TOCTOU barrier
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding

ETAPA815 rechecked hash-addressed evidence documents, but readiness authorities
outside `evidenceFileBytes` remained exposed to the same long-check window. This
included the release configuration, release-evidence root, requirements,
operator intent, source manifest/runbook, contract sources, optional project
configuration and compiled artifact/source inputs. A path could change after its
parsed value affected assessment and before the final result.

Correction

- All direct project reads now pass through one capture boundary keyed by path.
- A repeated read during collection must equal the first captured Buffer.
- The final barrier rereads every captured authority through the raw safe reader
  after child collection and assessment, immediately before output.
- Contract sources and every compiled-artifact source were moved onto the same
  boundary. Forge/Git executable identity checks remain independent and intact.
