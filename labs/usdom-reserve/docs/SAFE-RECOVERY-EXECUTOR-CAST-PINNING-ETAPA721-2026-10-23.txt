ETAPA 721 — recoverable Safe executor Cast pinning
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
The recoverable-Safe execution ceremony resolved Cast through PATH for Ledger relayer identity,
all multi-RPC observations, raw checks and three `swapOwner` broadcasts. Replacement after the
single TTY authorization could redirect the publication executable.

Correction
==========
The executor now resolves Cast once to an absolute canonical path and requires a regular
executable with one hard link, safe root/current ownership, non-group/world-writable file and
parent directory. It snapshots device/inode/size/mtime/ctime and SHA-256.

The bounded RPC helper and Ledger identity calls use only the captured path. Cast is rechecked
before initial Ledger identity, immediately after the exact three-swap TTY phrase, before the
post-consent relayer identity check and immediately before every role publication. Existing
multi-RPC target consensus, nonce/Safe nonce, code/state, raw transaction/envelope/hash,
source-snapshot TOCTOU, durable validation/evidence and no-clobber controls remain.

Node and JQ remain separately auditable command dependencies. Cast was prioritized because it
performs the RPC publication and Ledger identity operations.

Validation
==========
Structural test passes 1/1. Node syntax, `bash -n` and git diff checks pass. The executor and no
child, RPC, Ledger or Cast operation was run.

No signing, transmission, deployment, funds, build, Cargo, Rust, Solidity, RISC0 proof, staging,
commit, tag, publication or push was performed.
