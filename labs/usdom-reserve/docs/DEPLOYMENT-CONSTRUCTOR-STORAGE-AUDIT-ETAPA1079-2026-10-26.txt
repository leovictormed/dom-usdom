Scope
- Statically audited ProofReserve and Registry constructor ABI ordering,

Findings
1. The postdeployment preflight still declared DOMCheckpointRegistry.candidate()
   with the retired 11-field ABI. The live getter has 13 tuple fields because
   totalIssued and totalBurned precede confirmationHeight. ABI decoding could
   consequently assign economic totals to later fields and invalidate the
   intended empty-candidate attestation.
2. Cold-start observation omitted new terminal/economic state: reserve
   depositsClosed and gracefullyShutdown; registry gracefullyShutdown,
   latestIssuanceClosed, current/previous issued and burned totals; and candidate
   issued/burned totals plus candidateIssuanceClosed.
3. The source-verified reserve artifact KAT was stale after the audited contract
   hardening. The checked artifact was newer than and source-matched the current
   contract, but the expected runtime hash, initcode hash and sizes still named
   the prior build.
4. Permanent proved-debt storage occupies a new full word without altering the
   packed closure bit offset. Under the current declaration order totalDeposited
   is slot 11, while the queueEpoch/paused/depositsClosed word is slot 18 and
   depositsClosed remains bit 72. Any future issuance-closure manifest must use
   those release-specific slots and the exact runtime hash.

Corrections
- Updated the Registry candidate ABI to all 13 fields and cold-start observation
  to collect and reject nonzero/nonfalse economic, closure and terminal state.
- Updated exact observation schemas and negative mutation tests for every new

- Updated the source-verified reserve runtime/initcode KAT to the current exact

  f7cb8613...b538f and initcode size 29712. Added ABI assertions for permanent
  debt and terminal getters.

  constructor argument.
