Conclusion
==========

No new material production defect was found. All six token-policy observations use
the same bounded one-word STATICCALL decoder with TOKEN_GAS_LIMIT = 500,000. A token
getter that never returns exhausts only that allowance, fails closed as
UnsupportedToken, and cannot leave a partial reserve transition.

Coverage added
==============

deprecated(), upgradedAddress(), basisPointsRate(), maximumFee(), and
isBlackListed(address). The regression exercises every getter during deposit and the
recipient blacklist getter during payout. It proves:

- a failed policy observation cannot consume a deposit ID or create collateral credit;
- failed payout policy checks cannot mark a burn paid, reduce pending debt, or spend
  rate-limit capacity; and
- exact restoration permits the original payout to complete.
