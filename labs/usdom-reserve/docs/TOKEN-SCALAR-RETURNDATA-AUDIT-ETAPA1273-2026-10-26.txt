USDOM Solidity adversarial audit - stage 1273
Date: 2026-10-26
Scope: ERC-20 scalar returndata boundaries in USDOMProofReserve

Conclusion
==========

No new material production defect was found. The existing reserve fails closed when
the configured token returns malformed data from decimals() during construction or
balanceOf() during deposit and payout. The exercise added executable regression
coverage only; production Solidity and its ABI were not changed in this stage.

Boundary reviewed
=================

The hostile token can now return, independently for decimals() and balanceOf():

1. 31 bytes (truncated ABI word);
2. 33 bytes (trailing data);
3. a 1 MiB returndata bomb; or
4. one exact ABI word containing the adversarial value 2.

The constructor rejects all four decimals responses through the reserve's bounded
one-word STATICCALL decoder and the exact six-decimal comparison. Deposit and payout
use the same decoder for balanceOf and reject all four responses. Returndata must be
exactly 32 bytes, the caller copies at most one word, and failed token observations
revert the complete transaction.

Regression evidence
===================

testTokenDecimalsAndBalanceReturnDataMustBeExactAndBounded proves that:

- malformed decimals() data cannot create a reserve with an ambiguous token policy;
- malformed balanceOf() data cannot create deposit credit or consume a deposit ID;
- the same failures during payout cannot mark a burn paid, remove its pending debt,
  or change totalPendingAmount; and
- after exact token behavior is restored, the original deposit and withdrawal paths
  remain executable, so failure handling does not strand otherwise valid state.
