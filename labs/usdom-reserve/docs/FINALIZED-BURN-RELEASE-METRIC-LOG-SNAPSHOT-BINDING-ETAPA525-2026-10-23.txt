USD0M stage 525 — finalized-burn release metric-log snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The release-specific finalized-burn E2E runner reopened prover and compression
resource logs after artifact collection, then retained their parsed values
across runtime observation and isolated EVM execution without a common immutable
generation or final origin recheck.

Correction
==========

Before observation or Anvil, both logs are durably captured in an owner-only
replica. Every installed snapshot is reread for exact byte and SHA-256 equality,
and metrics are parsed only from captured buffers. After isolated EVM execution
and before report validation/publication, both original logs are reread and must
still match the captured bytes and hashes. The validated report remains durable
before the succeeded run-status completion marker.

Evidence and scope
==================

Focused structural tests enforce ordering and complete catalog coverage. A
direct concurrency test snapshots both logs, replaces one origin, and proves
the final recheck rejects it. Readiness requires
`production_finalized_burn_release_metric_logs_snapshotted_and_origins_rechecked=true`.
No proof, Anvil, network, signing, deployment, funds operation, commit or push
was run.
