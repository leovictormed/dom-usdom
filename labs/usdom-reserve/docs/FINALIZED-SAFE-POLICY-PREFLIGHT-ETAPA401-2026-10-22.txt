USDOM stage 401 — finalized Safe policy preflight
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

The contracts introduced continuous Safe policy enforcement in stage 400, but
the deployment preflight observed only each proxy singleton at its finalized
snapshot. Threshold, owners and modules were checked in separate Safe evidence,
so the machine deployment gate did not independently demonstrate that the exact
snapshot accepted for deployment would also satisfy the constructor.

Correction
==========

The mainnet preflight collector now reads masterCopy(), getThreshold(),
getOwners() and getModulesPaginated() for reserve governance, reserve guardian
and checkpoint guardian at the same explicit finalized block tag. Two pinned
independent Ethereum RPC sources must return byte-identical observations.

The strict preflight schema carries one policy record per role. Assessment
requires threshold 2, exactly three distinct nonzero canonical owner addresses,
an empty first module page and the exact Safe sentinel as its terminal cursor.
Missing, extra, malformed, duplicate or weakened fields fail closed. These
checks apply before deployment and again at the paused cold-start checkpoint.

funds, mining, project commit, tag or push occurred. The detached capacity proof
