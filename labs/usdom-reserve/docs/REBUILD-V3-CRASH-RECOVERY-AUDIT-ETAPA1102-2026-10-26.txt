ETAPA 1102 — REBUILD V3 CRASH / RECOVERY AUDIT
Date: 2026-10-26

Two concrete generation-confusion gaps were closed. First, a receipts output
already tracked in the pinned source snapshot could be accepted after a producer
returned success, allowing a valid receipt from an earlier generation to enter
the new verification chain. Receipts outputs must now be absent before their
producer runs; verify-only outputs already had the same create-once rule.

Second, hashing a tool before and after pathname-based execution left a narrow
hash-to-exec inode substitution window. Every producer, verify-only checker and
aggregator is now opened O_NOFOLLOW, checked as executable regular single-link,
hashed through its retained descriptor and executed through /proc/self/fd with
that descriptor inherited. Its inode/size/timestamps are checked again after
exit. Path replacement cannot select the executed bytes.

The existing temporary-candidate/atomic-publication design never resumes an
orphan after a crash. New tests demonstrate that an orphan containing a valid
old receipt remains untouched and cannot enter a fresh candidate, a prior
receipt in the source snapshot is rejected, verify-only cannot roll back the
authenticated predecessor, and an aliased executable inode is rejected.

Python compilation and the full lightweight suite pass 20/20; the focused
executable-inode regression passes 1/1 after its addition. Diff check passes.
