USD-native / USDOM — Stage 428: proof selector at value entrypoints
Date: 2026-10-22

Finding
=======

After stage 427, reserve and checkpoint entrypoints rejected noncanonical proof
lengths but still passed a 260-byte seal with an arbitrary four-byte selector
through their control checks to the immutable adapter. The adapter rejected it,
so this did not grant authority, but the outer executable boundary remained
broader than the signed RISC Zero Groth16 v3 policy.

Correction
==========

USDOMProofReserve.queueWithdrawal and
DOMCheckpointRegistry.submitCheckpointCandidate now require both the canonical
260-byte size and the exact 0x73c457ba RISC Zero Groth16 v3 selector before any
verifier interaction. The selector constants are private to avoid adding an
unneeded runtime getter. Exact-length wrong-selector proofs are tested to leave
reserve accounting and checkpoint candidate state unchanged.
