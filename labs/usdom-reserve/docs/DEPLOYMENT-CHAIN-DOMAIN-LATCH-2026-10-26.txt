DEPLOYMENT CHAIN DOMAIN LATCH — 2026-10-26

Finding
=======

The RISC Zero verifier adapters permanently bind their deployment chain ID and
reject proofs after a chain-ID change, but USDOMProofReserve used the live
block.chainid for deposit IDs and all burn hashes. After a chain-ID change the
reserve could still accept new collateral when its control accounts were direct
contracts, even though the production verifier could never authorize its later
redemption. The same change also altered the hash of an already queued debt, so
executeWithdrawal would reject it with PendingMismatch.

Fix
===

The reserve now stores deploymentChainId as an immutable. depositId, BurnIntent,
FinalizedBurnDebt and FinalizedBurn statements use that immutable domain rather
than a mutable environmental read. New deposits and first-time burn debts call
_requireDeploymentChain and fail with ChainChanged if the live chain ID differs.

Already queued debts remain executable because their exact statement identity
does not change. This is deliberate: an environmental fork must not turn an
already authenticated collateral obligation into an unpayable record. No new
exposure can be admitted on the divergent chain.

Security properties
===================

The immutable domain preserves cross-chain separation: it is the chain ID at
deployment and is already part of the reserve configuration identities. The
exact reserve address remains in every burn domain. A live chain-ID change does
not silently migrate the deployment to another domain.

Validation
==========

The regression changes the Foundry chain ID and proves that deposit ID and burn
statement hashes remain unchanged, fresh deposit and fresh debt revert with
ChainChanged, and the previously queued withdrawal still pays exactly once.
Existing hash-domain tests were updated to assert the deployment-chain model.

The complete offline Forge campaign passes: 201 tests across 14 suites, zero
failures. USDOMProofReserve runtime is 20,903 bytes, leaving 3,673 bytes under
EIP-170. DOMCheckpointRegistry remains 10,418 bytes. Whitespace validation
passes.

No Rust/Cargo, operational script, real network, signing, deployment, funds,
commit or push was used.
