ETAPA 818 — deployment, Safe and signer descriptor isolation
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======
Deployment, Safe recovery and the three Ledger signing ceremonies sanitized the
environment but still accepted caller-owned descriptors above stderr. They also
kept interactive stdin attached after the last consent/device-selection prompt,
including while raw transactions and signature bundles were validated and
installed.

Correction
==========
The two executors, the release/native shared signer, checkpoint signer, Safe
recovery signer and both release/native wrappers now close caller-owned fds >2
before resolving or executing a child. TTY stdin remains available only through
the final required consent/Ledger-selection prompt. Immediately afterward it is
replaced by /dev/null before raw/signature validation, installation or publish.
The executors' later fd 9 is ceremony-owned and intentionally remains open to
hold the evidence-directory flock; it never carries raw/signature bytes.

Shell authorization/signature variables are not exported. Explicit installer
redirections continue to carry only their intended immutable draft file.

Validation
==========
- Local /proc/fd harness injects fd 9 plus unread consent bytes and proves that
  an authority child receives neither.
- Structural checks bind closure before command resolution and stdin revocation
  after every final prompt, with no later interactive read.
- Focused descriptor/environment/pinning/durability/cleanup suites: 18/18 pass.
- bash -n: seven changed entrypoints pass.
- git diff --check: pass.

No Ledger, RPC, signing, deployment, transmission or fund operation ran.
