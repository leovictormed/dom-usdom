Findings and corrections
1. The full production release-core producer still discarded both adapter
   initcode hashes after deriving them from source-verified artifact bindings.
   Its pure reconciliation boundary now requires runtimeCodeHash, initcodeHash
   and configHash as one exact identity and materializes verifierInitcodeHash
   and checkpointVerifierInitcodeHash. The fixed wrapper requires and reports
   both values, so a runtime/config-compatible but different creation artifact
   cannot enter the release.
2. The deployed checkpoint registry preflight did not call or reconcile the new
   guardianOwnersCommitment getter. The collector ABI and exact observation
   schema now include it, and assessment compares it with the commitment
   recomputed from the release's canonical ordered owner trio. Missing, stale,
   malformed, reordered or substituted owner identity fails closed.
3. The release/preactivation, deployment plan, reproducible-build reports and
   readiness paths were traced end-to-end. Adapter initcode hashes are now
   present at both root materializers, deployment transaction input hashes,
   reproducible-build evidence and compiled readiness comparisons. Submission
   configurations independently recompute the current reserve/registry config
   hashes; the registry config includes checkpoint guardian owners.
4. Global active-code/test search found no surviving ETAPA648/intermediate
   reserve or registry vectors. Current differential vectors remain those
   recorded by ETAPA652 after ETAPA651/661.
