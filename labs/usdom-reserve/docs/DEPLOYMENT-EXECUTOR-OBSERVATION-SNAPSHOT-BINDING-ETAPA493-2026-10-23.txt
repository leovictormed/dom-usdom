Stage 493 — Deployment executor observation-snapshot binding
Date: 2026-10-23

The deployment executor still passed the caller's original identity-observation
pathname into every initial, presign, prepublish and prereceipt handoff. Its
SHA-256 was package-bound and substitutions failed, but the pathname remained
mutable across human delays and could cause cross-phase denial or inconsistent
file identity.

The executor now directly snapshots the owner-only observation into the same
private temporary ceremony boundary as the package. Before any handoff it
recomputes the snapshot SHA-256 and compares it with the authenticated package's
observationDocumentSha256 commitment. Every later phase receives only this
snapshot path. Replacement, omission, redirection and pathname TOCTOU after
startup cannot affect the ceremony.

`production_deployment_executor_observation_snapshotted` is a structural fact.
No network access, hardware signature, transaction, funds, mining, commit or
push occurred while producing this evidence.

Validation: complete operator runbook 71/71; readiness 60/60.
