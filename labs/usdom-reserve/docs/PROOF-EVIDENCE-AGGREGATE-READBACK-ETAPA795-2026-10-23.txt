ETAPA 795 — finalized-burn/checkpoint aggregate post-completion readback
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
Finalized-burn and checkpoint-capacity evidence materializers already published their immutable report precursor before the release-evidence aggregate and used durable writers with per-file exact readback. Identical precursor-only crash states were retryable. Like the RISC Zero catalog before ETAPA793, however, neither materializer revalidated all authority inputs and read both installed files after the aggregate completion marker.

Correction
For both materializers, immediately after the aggregate write:
- revalidate the Git/source boundary;
- re-read release and verifier configuration while accepting only the exact newly prepared release-evidence aggregate;
- revalidate the captured proof/capacity run origins;
- exact-read the immutable report with fixed length/private-file checks;
- exact-read the installed release-evidence aggregate;
- compare both files with the prepared generation and fail closed on any mismatch.

Crash behavior
A precursor-only crash remains exact-or-reject retryable. A completed call now proves source/run stability and one exact two-file generation after its completion marker. Concurrent substitution or a mixed generation rejects.
