USDOM stage 585 — independent signed deployment binding
Date: 2026-10-23

Finding
=======

The source-derived deployment package already checked the approved initcode
hash and the deterministic CREATE address. The final signed-transaction
validator compared the exact initcode and deployment fields, but trusted the
package's expected contract address and did not itself recompute the package's
initcode commitment. This made the last pre-publication boundary dependent on
the correctness of an earlier validation layer.

Correction
==========

`sdk/mainnet-deployment-execution.mjs` now:

* accepts only a canonical decimal uint64 nonce;
* canonicalizes and rejects zero deployer/contract identities;
* requires canonical lowercase, nonempty, even-length initcode;
* requires a canonical lowercase approved initcode hash and recomputes it;
* independently derives the CREATE address from deployer and nonce; and
* binds each numeric execution index to its canonical contract role name; and
* uses those validated values when comparing the recovered signed transaction.
